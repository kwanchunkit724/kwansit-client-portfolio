About e-print Group

e-print Group Limited (Promise Network Printing Limited) is a Hong Kong-listed company dedicated to delivering innovative digital printing and imaging solutions. With a comprehensive suite of brands and platforms, the Group serves a diverse client base spanning individual consumers, SMEs, and large corporations.

The Group's multi-brand strategy allows it to address different market segments effectively — from everyday business printing and large-format banners to personalised gifts and photo products — across Hong Kong and Malaysia.
