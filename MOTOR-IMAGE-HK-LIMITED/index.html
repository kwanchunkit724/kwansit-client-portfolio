Subaru's Core Technology

Unlike conventional AWD systems, Subaru's Symmetrical AWD features a perfectly balanced drivetrain layout — pairing seamlessly with the horizontally-opposed Boxer engine for a lower centre of gravity, superior traction, and responsive handling in all road conditions.

Subaru Technology

Engineered for Every Journey

Subaru's unique combination of proprietary technologies sets every model apart in performance, safety and efficiency.

⚙️

Boxer Engine

The horizontally-opposed flat engine lowers the centre of gravity and improves weight balance for superior handling.

🔄

Symmetrical AWD

Full-time Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive provides balanced power distribution for traction on any surface, in any weather.

👁️

EyeSight Driver Assist

Stereo camera-based pre-collision braking, adaptive cruise control, and lane-keep assist for confident, safe driving.

🌿

e-BOXER Hybrid

Electric motor-assisted Boxer engine combines improved fuel efficiency with responsive performance and reduced emissions.

🏔️

X-MODE

Optimises AWD, engine output, braking and transmission for steep descents and slippery off-road terrain.

🛡️

Subaru Global Platform

The rigid, low-centre-of-gravity architecture underpinning every Subaru model for improved safety and dynamics.
