Safety & Quality

Safety Is Non-Negotiable

At Martin Construction, the safety of our workers, clients, and the public is the highest priority on every site. We maintain a comprehensive Site Safety Plan on all projects and enforce strict compliance with the Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance and Buildings Ordinance requirements.

Our site supervisors hold current COSH (Certificate of Competence for Safety Supervisors) qualifications, and all workers complete mandatory safety inductions before commencing work. Regular toolbox talks reinforce safety awareness throughout the project lifecycle.

Quality management is equally rigorous — all works are subject to inspection and testing protocols, with non-conformance reports tracked to closure. We believe quality is built in, not inspected in.
