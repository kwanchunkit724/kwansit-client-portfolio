LLP Registration

Registering a Limited Liability Partnership in Hong Kong

The Limited Liability Partnerships Ordinance (Cap. 637) provides a flexible business structure combining partnership features with limited liability protection. We make the process straightforward.

1
Choose Your Structure

Determine whether an LLP suits your business needs — ideal for professional firms such as law, accounting, architecture, and consultancy practices requiring limited liability protection.

2
Name Reservation

Reserve your LLP name with the Companies Registry. We check name availability and ensure compliance with naming requirements under the LLP Ordinance.

3
Partnership Deed Preparation

Draft a comprehensive LLP agreement setting out capital contributions, profit-sharing arrangements, management responsibilities, and partner rights and obligations.

4
Registration Submission

Submit the completed registration application and documentation to the Companies Registry, along with the prescribed registration fee.

5
Post-Registration Compliance

Obtain Business Registration Certificate, set up partner accounts, and establish ongoing compliance procedures to keep your LLP in good standing.

Key Facts About HK Limited Liability Partnerships

✓

Legal Framework: Governed by the Limited Liability Partnerships Ordinance (Cap. 637), enacted to enable flexible professional partnerships with liability protection.

✓

Liability Protection: Partners' personal assets are protected from the negligence claims arising from acts of other partners or employees.

✓

Minimum Partners: At least two partners required. There is no upper limit on the number of partners in an LLP.

✓

Designated Partner: At least one Designated Partner (DP) must be appointed to manage compliance and filing obligations.

✓

Annual Filing: LLPs must file annual statements with the Companies Registry confirming partners and the Designated Partner details.

✓

Flexibility: Partners have the freedom to arrange internal governance as they see fit through the LLP agreement.
