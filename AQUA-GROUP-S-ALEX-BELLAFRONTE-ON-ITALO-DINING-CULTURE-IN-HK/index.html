About the Hotel

Iconic Harbourfront
Luxury Since 1989

Grand Hyatt Hong Kong stands as one of Asia's most iconic luxury hotels, commanding a prestigious position at 1 Harbour Road, Wan Chai — directly facing the magnificent Victoria Harbour. Since opening in 1989, the hotel has set the benchmark for five-star hospitality in Hong Kong.

Superbly connected to the Hong Kong Convention & Exhibition Centre, the hotel is the destination of choice for discerning travellers, international business leaders, and those celebrating life's most meaningful occasions. Every visit is defined by the seamless blend of contemporary luxury and warm, attentive service that has become the Grand Hyatt hallmark.
