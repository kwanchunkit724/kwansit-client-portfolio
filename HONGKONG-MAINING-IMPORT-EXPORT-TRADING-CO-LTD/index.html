About Us

Hong Kong's Gateway to Global Garment Supply Chains

"Connecting China's manufacturing excellence with the world's garment industry — one reliable shipment at a time."
— HongKong Maining Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd.

HongKong Maining Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd. is a professional international trading company registered and operating in Hong Kong. Leveraging Hong Kong's strategic position as Asia's premier trading hub, we specialise in sourcing, quality control, and export of garment accessories and industrial packaging materials.

Our core expertise lies in serving the textile and apparel supply chain — supplying packaging boxes, hang tags, paper stickers, polypropylene strings, and related accessories to garment manufacturers and textile mills in South Asia, Southeast Asia, and beyond.

With consistent trade activity exceeding 9,600 documented shipments annually, Maining is a high-volume, reliable partner for buyers who demand punctual delivery and product consistency.
