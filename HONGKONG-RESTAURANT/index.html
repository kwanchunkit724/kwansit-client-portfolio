About

Hong Kong's Premier Hotel Dining Destination

Grand Hyatt Hong Kong stands as one of the city's most iconic luxury hotels, located on the Wan Chai waterfront with sweeping views of Victoria Harbour. Its collection of restaurants and bars has long been a destination in its own right — drawing discerning diners, business travellers, and food lovers from across Asia and beyond.

Opened in 1989, Grand Hyatt Hong Kong has built a reputation for culinary excellence across multiple dining concepts under one roof. From the refined Cantonese artistry of One Harbour Road to the convivial energy of the Grand Café and the sophistication of its iconic Champagne Bar, every venue offers a distinctive experience.

The hotel's Food & Beverage team specialises in tailored private dining, corporate events, and celebratory occasions — with dedicated event spaces ranging from intimate private rooms to grand ballroom experiences overlooking the harbour.

Restaurants & Bars

Six Distinctive Dining Experiences

Every restaurant at Grand Hyatt Hong Kong has been crafted to deliver a unique culinary journey — whether you're seeking classic Cantonese dim sum, Italian pasta, or a nightcap with panoramic harbour views.

Cantonese Fine Dining
One Harbour Road
Grand Hyatt Hong Kong's flagship Cantonese restaurant, celebrated for its refined dim sum, masterful Cantonese roast dishes, and sweeping Victoria Harbour views from the 8th floor. A perennial Michelin-recognised institution in the city's dining scene.
Signature: Dim Sum, Cantonese Roast, Seafood Location: 8/F, Grand Hyatt Hong Kong
Michelin Recognised
Italian Fine Dining
Grissini
Hong Kong's long-standing Italian fine dining favourite, featuring authentic regional Italian cuisine in an elegant, art-filled dining room. Known for its handmade pasta, premium imported ingredients, and extensive Italian wine cellar.
Signature: Handmade Pasta, Risotto, Tiramisu Location: 2/F, Grand Hyatt Hong Kong
Awarded Wine List
All-Day International
Grand Café
A beloved all-day dining destination offering a lavish international buffet spread alongside à la carte options. Grand Café is known for its legendary Sunday brunch — an indulgent spread of fresh seafood, live cooking stations, and desserts.
Signature: Sunday Brunch, Seafood, Live Stations Location: Lobby Level, Grand Hyatt Hong Kong
Famous Sunday Brunch
Steakhouse
The Grill by Hyatt
A classic steakhouse experience featuring premium dry-aged and wagyu cuts, grilled to perfection over open flame. Complemented by an extensive whisky and wine programme and intimate, club-like atmosphere.
Signature: Wagyu, Dry-Aged Beef, Seafood Tower Location: Grand Hyatt Hong Kong
Premium Steaks
Champagne & Cocktail Bar
Champagne Bar
One of Hong Kong's most iconic bars, celebrated for its unrivalled champagne selection — over 80 champagne labels — alongside signature cocktails and live entertainment. A favourite after-work and evening destination since the hotel's opening in 1989.
Signature: Champagne, Classic Cocktails, Live Music Location: Lobby Level, Grand Hyatt Hong Kong
80+ Champagnes
Wellness Café
Plateau Spa Café
Adjacent to the award-winning Plateau Spa, this serene café offers health-conscious cuisine, cold-pressed juices, herbal teas, and light bites designed to complement the wellness experience. Perfect for post-spa refreshment or a peaceful morning retreat.
Signature: Healthy Bowls, Juices, Herbal Infusions Location: Plateau Spa, Grand Hyatt Hong Kong

Dining Experiences

Beyond the Restaurant

Grand Hyatt Hong Kong's F&B team creates bespoke experiences for every occasion — from intimate private dinners to large-scale corporate galas.

🎂
Private Dining
Exclusive private dining rooms available at One Harbour Road and Grissini, accommodating from 8 to 60 guests for intimate celebrations, corporate dinners, and milestone events with personalised menus.
🥂
Champagne Brunches
Grand Café's legendary Sunday champagne brunch features unlimited champagne, fresh oysters, live carving stations, and artisanal desserts — a weekly celebration loved by Hong Kong families and visitors alike.
🌸
Seasonal & Festive Menus
Special menus crafted for Chinese New Year, Mid-Autumn Festival, Christmas, and Valentine's Day — highlighting seasonal ingredients and traditional recipes reimagined by the hotel's executive chefs.
🏢
Corporate Events
Full-service catering for business functions from boardroom lunches to gala dinners, with dedicated event coordinators, audiovisual support, and customised menus for groups of 20 to 1,000.
🍷
Wine & Sake Dinners
Curated wine pairing dinners hosted throughout the year, featuring visiting winemakers, sommelier-led tastings, and exclusive multi-course menus designed to complement fine wine and sake selections.
🎁
Takeaway & Gift Hampers
Signature mooncake collections, Chinese New Year gift sets, premium festive hampers, and takeaway delicacies from One Harbour Road — available seasonally and for corporate gifting year-round.
