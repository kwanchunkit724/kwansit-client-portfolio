About ONC

A Trusted Name in Hong Kong Law

With continuous growth since our establishment in 1992, ONC Lawyers has become one of the largest local law firms in Hong Kong, with more than 130 members of legally-qualified and supporting staff.

Our firm is a member of the International Society of Primerus Law Firms — an international network of the world's finest law firms. With seamless support from 200 member firms in more than 40 countries, we assist our clients and serve their needs in major jurisdictions worldwide.

Recognised for excellence across a wide range of practice areas, ONC Lawyers brings together experienced practitioners dedicated to delivering the highest quality legal advice and services to businesses, institutions, and individuals in Hong Kong and across the region.
