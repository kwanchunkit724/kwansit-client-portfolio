FDUSD Stablecoin

Powering the First Digital USD Stablecoin

First Digital USD (FDUSD) is a USD-backed stablecoin issued with First Digital Trust Limited as the designated reserve manager and custodian. Launched in 2023, FDUSD is designed to maintain a 1:1 peg with the US Dollar, backed by high-quality liquid assets held in segregated trust accounts.

As the licensed trust company and reserve custodian, First Digital Trust ensures that all FDUSD in circulation is fully backed by US Treasury bills, cash equivalents and other approved high-quality liquid assets, held in segregated accounts separate from First Digital's own assets.

Regular independent attestations confirm the full reserve backing of FDUSD, providing transparency and confidence to institutional users, exchanges and DeFi protocols that integrate FDUSD as a settlement and liquidity instrument.
