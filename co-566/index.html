About Ming Kee

Paving Hong Kong
Since 1993

🏗️ Hong Kong's No.1 Asphalt Company

Founded in 1993, Ming Kee Company Ltd. (明基服務有限公司) has spent over three decades building its reputation as Hong Kong's most experienced and best-equipped asphalt engineering firm. Today, we operate the largest construction team and most comprehensive fleet of professional asphalt machinery in Hong Kong.

Over 30 years of continuous operation has given us deep expertise in all aspects of asphalt engineering — from large-scale public infrastructure and private housing estate paving to sports track construction and specialist milling works.

Our team of experienced professionals, combined with state-of-the-art paving and milling equipment, allows us to tackle projects of any scale with the precision and reliability that Hong Kong's construction industry demands.
