About DiningCity

The World's Restaurant Week Platform

Connecting food lovers with great restaurants worldwide through curated dining events

DiningCity is the global platform behind Restaurant Week events in cities around the world, including Hong Kong. Since launching Restaurant Week in Hong Kong, DiningCity has connected hundreds of thousands of diners with the city's finest restaurants through specially curated menus at exceptional value.

The platform's mission is simple: to make great dining accessible, to help food lovers discover new restaurants, and to support Hong Kong's vibrant restaurant industry with a surge of engaged, passionate diners during each Restaurant Week period.
