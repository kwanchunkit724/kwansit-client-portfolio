About Us
Hong Kong's Leader in Luxury Real Estate

Landscope Realty Limited has established itself as the foremost name in Hong Kong's luxury property market, combining local expertise with a world-class global network.

Our Story & Values

Landscope Realty Limited is Hong Kong's leading luxury property brokerage, known for its unrivalled track record in the city's most prestigious addresses — from The Peak and Repulse Bay to the Midlevels and Deep Water Bay.

Having brokered Hong Kong's highest residential sale at HK$728.88 million, our expertise in the ultra-luxury segment is unmatched. Our team specialises in connecting ultra-high-net-worth clients with the finest properties Hong Kong has to offer — as well as premium opportunities worldwide through our Christie's International Real Estate network.

Under the leadership of Managing Director K.S. Koh, our firm operates on three core principles: integrity, professionalism, and discretion — the hallmarks of every transaction we handle.

🎯

Integrity

Every transaction is handled with complete transparency and honesty. We build relationships for life, not just for a single deal.

🏅

Professionalism

Our licensed team brings deep market knowledge, meticulous due diligence, and seamless execution to every assignment.

Discretion

Global Reach
