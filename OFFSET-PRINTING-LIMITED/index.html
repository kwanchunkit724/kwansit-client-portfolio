PRODUCTS & SERVICES

Professional Offset Printing Solutions

Attis Offset Printing provides quality offset print production for a wide range of product categories — from packaging and stationery to diaries, organisers, and school supplies.

🖨️
Offset Printing
Professional commercial offset printing for high-volume production runs. Consistent colour quality, sharp detail, and cost-effective output for all formats and paper stocks.
📦
Packaging Materials
Custom printed packaging solutions including boxes, cartons, wrappers, and sleeves. Ideal for retail products, gifts, and branded merchandise requiring attractive, durable packaging.
📓
Diaries, Address Books & Organisers
Bespoke diary, address book, and planner production for corporate gifting and retail. Custom covers, page layouts, binding styles, and branding options available.
✏️
Stationery & Office Supplies
Printed stationery and office supply products including notepads, writing pads, letterheads, envelopes, and branded office materials for businesses and retail distribution.
🎒
School Supplies
Exercise books, notebooks, and school stationery printing for educational institutions, publishers, and retail markets. Durable, affordable, and customisable for bulk orders.
🏭
OEM & Custom Production
OEM printing services for brands requiring private-label or custom-specification print products. Our Kowloon facility handles production from design to finished goods delivery.
