About Us

Your Trusted Global Freight Partner

DCL Global Logistics (HK) Limited (盈達國際物流(香港)有限公司) was established in 2008 and is headquartered at Kerry TC Warehouse 1 in Kwai Chung, Hong Kong. Over 15+ years, we have grown into a full-service international freight forwarder with offices across mainland China.

Our team of 50+ professionals brings over 20 years of combined logistics experience, delivering comprehensive air freight, sea freight, warehousing, trucking, freeze storage and dangerous goods solutions to clients across more than 200 countries worldwide.

As an ISO 9001 certified company, IATA member, and NVOCC-qualified operator in China, we maintain the highest standards of quality, compliance and customer service across all our operations.
