🏷️ Your Label Printing Partner

Advance Label Limited is a professional label and sticker printing company based in Hong Kong, serving clients across retail, F&B, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, logistics, and industrial sectors.

We offer end-to-end label solutions — from design consultation and material selection through digital or flexo printing, finishing, and delivery. Our team ensures every label meets your exact specifications for colour accuracy, adhesion, and durability.

Whether you need short-run digital labels or high-volume flexographic production, Advance Label delivers quality and consistency on every roll and sheet.
