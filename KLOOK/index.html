About Luxe Travel

Hong Kong's Luxury
Travel Authority

Luxe Travel is Hong Kong's leading specialist in luxury and bespoke travel experiences. We serve discerning clients who demand more than the ordinary — private villa retreats, exclusive cultural expeditions, tailored honeymoon escapes and ultra-luxury cruises across the world's most coveted destinations.

Our Philosophy

"Travel is not just about where you go — it's about how you experience it." Every Luxe Travel journey is meticulously crafted to reflect the unique preferences and aspirations of each client, ensuring memories that last a lifetime.

Based in Hong Kong, our team of seasoned travel consultants possesses intimate knowledge of the world's finest hotels, resorts and hidden gems — from the Maldives and French Riviera to Japan's private ryokans and Africa's exclusive safari lodges.

Trusted Expertise

Our consultants hold deep, personal knowledge of every destination we recommend.

24/7 Concierge

Personalised Service

Exclusive Access
