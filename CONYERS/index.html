About Conyers

A Century of Offshore Legal Excellence

Conyers Dill & Pearman was founded in Bermuda in 1928 and has grown into one of the world's preeminent offshore law firms. The firm advises some of the world's most sophisticated corporations, financial institutions, asset managers and high-net-worth individuals.

The Hong Kong office serves as the Asia-Pacific regional hub, providing specialist offshore legal counsel to clients across Greater China, Southeast Asia, Japan and Korea. The office works closely with Conyers' offices in BVI, Cayman Islands and Bermuda to deliver seamless cross-border advice.

Conyers Corporate Services provides corporate administration services for offshore entities, including company formation, registered agent and office services, corporate secretarial and compliance services across all three core jurisdictions.
