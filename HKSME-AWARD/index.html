About The Award

Celebrating Hong Kong's
Business Champions

🇭🇰 Hong Kong SMEs — The Pearl's Lustre

"Hong Kong SMEs are the lustre of pearl. Although small, they are bright and they make Hong Kong, the Pearl of the Orient, shine." The HKSME Award exists to celebrate these unsung champions of the Hong Kong economy.

The Hong Kong SME Award (HKSME Award) is a prestigious annual recognition programme jointly organised by the Hong Kong General Chamber of Commerce and the Hong Kong Productivity Council, with support from the Trade and Industry Department of the HKSAR Government.

SMEs in Hong Kong make up 98% of all corporations and are the major driving force of the city's economy. The Award recognises outstanding achievements, sets examples for other entrepreneurs, and motivates SMEs to continue contributing to Hong Kong's economic growth.

"SMEs in Hong Kong have always occupied an important place in our economy and have contributed significantly to the development of the industrial and services sectors. As engines powering the creation of employment, SMEs have increasingly been recognised as the linchpin of any productive economy."

— The Honourable Donald Tsang, Financial Secretary, HKSAR

Programme Objectives

The HKSME Award programme serves multiple important objectives for Hong Kong's business community:

1
Recognise and commend outstanding SMEs that demonstrate excellence in business operations, innovation, and competitiveness
2
Set successful business models as examples for other entrepreneurs and SMEs to benchmark and learn from
3
Create opportunities for SMEs to share knowledge, network, and learn from each other's experiences
4
Motivate SMEs to innovate, improve productivity, and contribute further to Hong Kong's economic development
5
Promote the importance of SMEs and raise public awareness of their vital contribution to the Hong Kong economy
Award Categories

Recognition Across All Sectors

The HKSME Award recognises excellence across key dimensions of SME performance and operation.

💡
Innovation Excellence
Recognising SMEs that have embraced innovation as a core productivity strategy across all aspects of their business operations, driving competitive advantage in the marketplace.
📈
Business Growth
Honouring enterprises that have achieved outstanding business growth, demonstrating strong financial performance, market expansion, and sustainable development strategies.
🌿
Sustainability & ESG
Celebrating SMEs that have integrated environmental, social, and governance principles into their business practices, contributing to a more sustainable Hong Kong economy.
👥
People Development
Recognising outstanding human resources management, employee development programmes, workplace culture, and talent retention strategies that make SMEs great places to work.
🌐
Market Expansion
Honouring SMEs that have successfully expanded into new markets, particularly those that have leveraged Hong Kong's position as a gateway to the mainland and international business.
⭐
Overall Excellence
The Grand Award for SMEs demonstrating outstanding all-round performance across multiple business dimensions, setting the highest standard of entrepreneurial achievement in Hong Kong.
Award Criteria

What We Look For

Winners are evaluated against rigorous criteria covering all aspects of business excellence.

💡
Innovation
Adoption of innovative practices and technologies to drive productivity
📊
Financial Performance
Sound financial management, sustainable growth, and profitability
🎯
Strategy
Clear business vision, strategy, and execution capability
🤝
Customer Focus
Commitment to quality service and customer satisfaction
