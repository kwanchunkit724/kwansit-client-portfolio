Trading Services

Our Trade Services

Beyond product supply, Patrickal Trading provides value-added services to support our customers' sourcing and procurement needs.

🔍
Fabric Sourcing
Access our extensive network of mills and suppliers in Greater China, Southeast Asia, and Europe to source specific fabrics to your technical specifications, quantity, and price requirements.
📦
Stock Wholesale
Ready-to-ship stock fabrics available for immediate purchase, ideal for small-batch orders, sampling, and urgent production requirements where lead time is critical.
🎯
Custom Development
Collaborate with our team on custom fabric development — from yarn selection and construction to dyeing, printing, and finishing — to create exclusive materials for your brand.
🚢
Import & Export
Experienced in international textile trade, we handle documentation, customs clearance, and logistics coordination for fabric shipments between Hong Kong, China, and global markets.
