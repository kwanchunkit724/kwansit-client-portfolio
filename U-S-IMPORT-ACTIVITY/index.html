About ImportInfo

U.S. Customs Trade Intelligence

ImportInfo.com is a specialized trade data platform providing access to U.S. import activity records sourced from U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) bills of lading. Our database covers millions of import records dating back to 2012, updated continuously as new manifests are filed.

Businesses use ImportInfo to conduct competitive intelligence, identify new suppliers, monitor competitor import activity, and research supply chain relationships. Our data covers importers, exporters, shippers, consignees, vessels, ports of entry, and commodity descriptions.

From tracking a specific company's import history to discovering which factories are supplying your competitors — ImportInfo delivers the trade transparency you need to make smarter business decisions.

📊 Sample Data

Example: BRUMATE LLC (Denver, CO) has 1,002 bills of lading on file between June 2018 and April 2026, with top associated shippers including HAERS HK LIMITED and EVERICH COMMERCE GROUP LIMITED in Hong Kong and mainland China.
