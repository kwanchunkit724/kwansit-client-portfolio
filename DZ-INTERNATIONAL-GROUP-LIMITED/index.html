Core Services

Comprehensive Logistics Services

From sea and air freight to warehousing and customs clearance, DZ International Group provides the full spectrum of logistics services to support your global trade operations.

🚢

Sea Freight

FCL and LCL ocean freight services to and from major ports worldwide. Competitive rates, reliable schedules, and expert cargo handling through our carrier network.

✈️

Air Freight

Express and standard air freight solutions for time-sensitive cargo. Direct and consolidated services from Hong Kong and major Asian gateways to global destinations.

🏭

Warehousing

Secure warehousing and distribution facilities in Hong Kong and across the region. Inventory management, cross-docking, and value-added services available.

📋

Customs Brokerage

Expert customs clearance and trade compliance services for imports and exports. Licensed brokers handle all documentation, tariff classification, and regulatory requirements.

🚛

Cross-Border Trucking

Road freight solutions between Hong Kong and Mainland China, facilitating seamless cross-border cargo movement through designated land crossings.

🔗

Supply Chain Solutions

Integrated supply chain management services — from origin pick-up to final delivery — with real-time tracking and dedicated account management support.

Industry Solutions

Tailored Trade Solutions

We understand that different industries have different logistics needs. Our solutions are designed to address the specific requirements of key trading sectors.

01

Retail & E-Commerce

End-to-end fulfilment solutions for retail brands and online sellers — including inventory storage, order processing, last-mile delivery, and returns management across Asia Pacific.

02

Manufacturing & Industrial

Raw material inbound logistics and finished goods export for manufacturers in China and across Asia — optimised for cost efficiency and production schedule alignment.

03

Perishables & Cold Chain

Temperature-controlled logistics for food, pharmaceutical, and perishable cargo — maintaining cold chain integrity from origin to destination with certified handling.

04

Project & Bulk Cargo

Specialist project logistics for heavy machinery, oversized cargo, and industrial equipment — including route survey, special transport permits, and on-site support.
