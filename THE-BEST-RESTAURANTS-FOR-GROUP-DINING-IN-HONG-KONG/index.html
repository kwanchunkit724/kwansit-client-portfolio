Our Story
Italian-American Fine Dining

The Art of the
Italian-American Table

Carbone brings the timeless glamour of mid-century Italian-American dining to the heart of Hong Kong. A restaurant where every detail — the tuxedoed service, the lavish red-sauce dishes, the theatrical ambience — conspires to transport guests to a golden era of dining.

From its origins in New York's Greenwich Village, Carbone has become one of the world's most celebrated restaurant brands. The Hong Kong outpost, nestled in Lan Kwai Fong, delivers that same singular experience: rich, indulgent Italian-American cuisine served with impeccable style.

Whether you're celebrating a milestone, hosting an intimate dinner, or simply seeking the finest pasta and veal in Central, Carbone Hong Kong delivers an evening to remember.
