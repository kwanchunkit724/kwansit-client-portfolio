About Us

A Century-Old Legacy, A Global Future

"Enrich the people, prosper Xinjiang, strengthen the country."
— Enterprise Mission, Xinjiang Zhongtai Chemical Group

Xinjiang Zhongtai Chemical Co., Ltd. is one of the Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region's key resource-conversion enterprises. Its roots trace back to the Xinjiang Caustic Soda Plant, established in 1958 — giving the group over 65 years of deep industrial expertise.

The group was officially established on December 18th, 2001, and listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange on December 8th, 2006. Zhong Tai International Development (HK) Limited serves as the group's gateway to global markets, managing international trade, export operations, and overseas partnerships from Hong Kong.

With a diversified portfolio spanning green chemicals, ecological cotton textiles, and supply chain services, Zhongtai's products reach buyers across 12+ cities and regions worldwide.
