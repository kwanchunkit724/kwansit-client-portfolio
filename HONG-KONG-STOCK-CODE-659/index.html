About NWS Holdings

A Foundation of
Infrastructure & Services

NWS Holdings Limited (新創建集團有限公司) is listed on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange under stock code 0659. The company is a member of the New World Group, one of Hong Kong's most respected diversified conglomerates.

With a focus on long-term value creation, NWS Holdings invests in and operates infrastructure and service assets that deliver essential connectivity, construction, logistics, and management capabilities to communities across Hong Kong and China.

The group's strategy centres on owning quality assets with stable, recurring income streams — from toll roads with long concession tenures to construction contracts with government clients and long-term facilities management agreements.
