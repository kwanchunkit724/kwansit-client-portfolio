About Anda

World-Class Intelligent
Equipment Manufacturer

Guangdong Anda Automation Solutions Co., Ltd. is a smart equipment and system manufacturer providing vertical integration of research & development, manufacturing, sales and service — specialising in fluid applications and intelligent automation systems.

Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Dongguan, China, Anda has grown into a global leader with offices in Hong Kong (established 2016), the United States, Mexico, Malaysia, and branch offices in Hunan, Shenzhen and Suzhou.

Anda is dedicated to delivering smart manufacturing products and services, committed to practicing with leading technologies, continuous innovation, integrity, and diligence — ensuring customer satisfaction is central to everything we do.

Product Lines

Core Product Series

Anda's precision automation equipment covers the full spectrum of fluid application and intelligent assembly systems for demanding industrial environments.

🎯

Precision Conformal Coating Machine Series

High-accuracy conformal coating systems for PCB protection in electronics manufacturing, ensuring consistent coverage and optimal circuit board longevity.

💉

Dispensing Machine Series

High-precision fluid dispensing equipment for adhesive bonding, potting, encapsulation and underfill applications across electronics and semiconductor manufacturing.

🔥

Curing Oven Series

Precision thermal curing ovens engineered for consistent temperature profiles, supporting UV and thermal curing processes in advanced electronics production lines.

⚡

Plasma Treatment System Series

Atmospheric and vacuum plasma treatment systems for surface activation, cleaning and etching — enhancing adhesion and material performance in critical processes.

🔧

Multi-functional Assembly Systems

Flexible automated assembly platforms integrating multiple process steps for high-efficiency smart manufacturing lines.

🔬

Semiconductor Manufacturing Equipment

Specialised equipment for semiconductor packaging and assembly processes, meeting the stringent requirements of the chip industry.

🏥

Medical Electronic Dispensing & Assembly

ISO-compliant dispensing and assembly machines for medical electronics, combining ultra-precision with full traceability and quality assurance.

🏭

Smart Manufacturing System Solutions

End-to-end Industry 4.0 solutions integrating equipment, software and data analytics for fully connected intelligent factories.
