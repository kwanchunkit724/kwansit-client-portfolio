Reliable Dry Bulk Transportation Worldwide

GH GLORY is a Panamax-class dry bulk vessel with a carrying capacity of approximately 74,973 DWT, owned and operated by the Group. Registered under the laws and flag of Hong Kong, the vessel provides worldwide marine transportation services for dry bulk cargoes.

The vessel operates under time charters for a contractual period, during which the Group provides and pays for all crew provisions, vessel insurance, and repair and maintenance costs. Charterers bear voyage-specific costs including bunker fees, port charges, and pilotage.

  • Panamax dry bulk carrier capable of navigating the Panama Canal
  • Registered under Hong Kong laws and flag
  • Experienced crew with high operational standards
  • Regular maintenance to ensure safe, reliable operations
  • Worldwide route capability for major dry bulk trade lanes
