Our Services

Comprehensive Healthcare Services

From routine check-ups to specialist consultations — Human Health provides a full spectrum of healthcare services under one roof.

👨‍⚕️
Medical Consultation
General practice and specialist consultations covering a wide range of medical conditions. Our doctors take time to understand your health needs and provide personalised care.
  • General Practice (GP)
  • Specialist Referrals
  • Chronic Disease Management
  • Follow-up Consultations
🔍
Health Screening
Comprehensive health check programmes tailored to your age, gender, and health history. Early detection is the best prevention — our doctors will recommend the right plan for you.
  • Basic Health Check
  • Executive Health Screening
  • Women's Health Screening
  • Cancer Screening
🔬
Diagnostic Imaging
Modern diagnostic imaging services to support accurate diagnosis and treatment planning. Results delivered promptly to support clinical decision-making.
  • X-Ray
  • Ultrasound
  • CT Scan
  • MRI
🧪
Laboratory Services
Comprehensive clinical laboratory testing from blood panels to specialised tests. Accurate, fast results to support timely diagnosis and monitoring of your health.
  • Blood Tests
  • Urine Analysis
  • Microbiological Tests
  • Tumour Markers
💉
Vaccination
Preventive vaccination services for all ages — from seasonal influenza to travel vaccines and childhood immunisation. Our medical team advises on the appropriate vaccines for your needs.
  • Influenza Vaccine
  • COVID-19 Vaccine
  • HPV Vaccine
  • Travel Vaccines
🏢
Corporate Health
Customised corporate health programmes for businesses and organisations. Group health screening, employee wellness, pre-employment medicals, and occupational health services.
  • Group Health Screening
  • Pre-Employment Medical
  • Workplace Wellness
  • Corporate Vaccination
