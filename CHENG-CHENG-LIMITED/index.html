OUR SERVICES

Professional Accounting & Advisory

Cheng & Cheng delivers a comprehensive range of accounting and corporate services tailored to the needs of Hong Kong businesses, from start-ups to established enterprises.

📊
Audit & Assurance
Statutory audits performed by licensed CPAs in full compliance with Hong Kong Standards on Auditing, providing assurance to stakeholders and satisfying IRD requirements.
📝
Tax Services
Profits tax filing, tax planning and advisory services for corporations and individuals. Expert guidance on Hong Kong tax law to optimise your tax position and ensure timely compliance.
📒
Accounting & Bookkeeping
Accurate and timely bookkeeping, management accounts and financial statement preparation, giving you a clear picture of your business performance throughout the year.
📋
Company Secretarial
Full company secretarial services including annual returns, board resolutions, statutory registers, and Companies Registry filings to keep your company in good standing.
🏢
Company Formation
Efficient Hong Kong company incorporation services including name check, Memorandum & Articles preparation, and Companies Registry submission to get your business started quickly.
💡
Business Advisory
Strategic financial advisory covering business structuring, due diligence, cashflow planning and CFO support, helping you make informed decisions for long-term business growth.
