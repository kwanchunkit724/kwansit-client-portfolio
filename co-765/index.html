OUR SERVICES

Specialised CPA Services for Your Business

At Kaida, we are dedicated to delivering accurate, reliable and top-quality financial services. We specialise in audit, tax advisory, corporate financing and corporate services tailored to meet your unique needs.

📊
Audit Services
Statutory audit engagements conducted by our licensed CPAs in accordance with Hong Kong Standards on Auditing. We deliver independent, credible audit reports that provide assurance to management, shareholders and regulators, supporting sound financial governance across your organisation.
📝
Tax Advisory
Comprehensive Hong Kong tax advisory services covering profits tax, salaries tax, stamp duty and cross-border tax structuring. We help you navigate complex tax regulations, optimise your tax position and ensure timely, accurate compliance with Inland Revenue Department requirements.
💼
Corporate Financing Services
Advisory support for businesses seeking financing solutions, including financial due diligence, business valuations and restructuring advice. We help you present your business in the best light to investors, lenders and strategic partners to secure the capital needed for growth.
📋
Corporate Services
End-to-end corporate services for Hong Kong companies including company incorporation, company secretarial maintenance, accounting and bookkeeping. We handle the administrative burden so you can focus on growing your business with confidence in its compliance and governance.
