About KKday

Asia's Largest
Travel Experiences Marketplace

KKday is Asia's leading online travel experiences marketplace, connecting travellers with unique local activities, tours, and attraction tickets across 100+ destinations worldwide. Since our founding in 2014, we've helped millions of travellers discover the world beyond hotel rooms and tourist traps.

🌟 Our Mission

We believe every journey should be an adventure. KKday curates authentic local experiences — from street food tours and cultural workshops to adrenaline-fuelled adventures — so you can truly immerse yourself in every destination you visit.

KKday's Hong Kong office, located in Kowloon Bay, serves Greater China and Asia-Pacific, offering curated experiences in Hong Kong, Macau, mainland China, Southeast Asia, Japan, Korea, and beyond.
