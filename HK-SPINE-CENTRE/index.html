About HK Spine Centre

Expert Spine Care at Gleneagles Hong Kong

A professorial-led, team-based spine programme delivering world-class orthopaedic care in a state-of-the-art private hospital setting.

The HK Spine Centre, located within Gleneagles Hospital Hong Kong in Wong Chuk Hang, provides comprehensive care for the full spectrum of spinal conditions — from common degenerative diseases to complex deformities and trauma.

Led by professors from The University of Hong Kong and supported by an experienced team of specialist consultants, the Centre operates on a professorial-led and team-based model, ensuring every patient benefits from both specialist expertise and multi-disciplinary collaboration.

Equipped with advanced robotic navigation technology, the Centre delivers precision-guided surgical interventions that minimise risk and promote faster recovery. Post-operative care includes structured physiotherapy and rehabilitation to support long-term outcomes.

Conditions We Treat

Comprehensive Spinal Condition Management

Our specialist team provides assessment, diagnosis, and treatment for a wide range of spinal disorders and injuries.

🔵

Disc Herniation

Bulging or ruptured intervertebral discs causing nerve compression, radiating pain, numbness or weakness in the arms or legs.

🔵

Spinal Stenosis

Narrowing of the spinal canal that puts pressure on the spinal cord or nerve roots, commonly causing leg pain and difficulty walking.

🔵

Scoliosis & Deformity

Abnormal lateral curvature of the spine, ranging from adolescent idiopathic scoliosis to adult degenerative deformity requiring correction.

🔵

Degenerative Spine Disease

Age-related wear and tear of spinal structures including disc degeneration, facet joint arthritis, and spondylolisthesis.

🔵

Spinal Trauma & Fractures

Vertebral fractures and spinal injuries resulting from trauma, osteoporosis, or other conditions requiring stabilisation or surgery.

🔵

Spinal Tumours

Primary and metastatic spinal tumours requiring specialised surgical and oncological management in coordination with the hospital's cancer services.

Our Services

Specialist Spine Services

From initial consultation through to post-operative recovery, our Centre offers a complete continuum of spinal care.

🩺

Specialist Consultation

Comprehensive assessment by HKU professors and specialist consultants to accurately diagnose your spinal condition and recommend appropriate treatment pathways.

🔬

Diagnostic Imaging

Advanced MRI, CT and X-ray imaging available in-hospital for precise diagnosis of spinal pathology, nerve involvement and structural abnormalities.

💊

Non-Surgical Treatment

Conservative management including medication, pain injections, nerve blocks, and guided physiotherapy for patients where surgery is not indicated.

🤖

Robotic-Assisted Surgery

State-of-the-art robotic navigation system enables precise pedicle screw placement and minimally invasive spinal procedures, reducing risk and recovery time.

🏃

Post-Op Rehabilitation

Structured physiotherapy and rehabilitation programme following spine surgery, designed to restore function, strength, and mobility as quickly as possible.

🚨

Emergency Assessment

Urgent spinal assessments available upon request for acute presentations including cauda equina syndrome, acute disc prolapse, or spinal trauma.
