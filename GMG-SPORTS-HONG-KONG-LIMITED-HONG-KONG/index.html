Who We Are

A Global Sports Retail Powerhouse

GMG Sports Hong Kong Limited is the Hong Kong entity of the GMG Group — a Dubai-headquartered, globally active conglomerate with operations spanning sports retail, everyday goods, health & beauty, properties, logistics, home, and ventures.

GMG's Sports Division is the group's flagship business, with a diversified portfolio of over 90 leading international sports brands and 500+ stores globally. In Hong Kong and Asia-Pacific, GMG operates premium multi-sports retail concepts and distributes the world's most respected athletic and lifestyle brands.

Inspiring and empowering active lifestyles is at the core of what we do — from cutting-edge performance gear to streetwear culture and beyond.
