Trading Services

How We Add Value

From factory sourcing to customs clearance, Fuxing Trade handles the full trading cycle so your business can focus on growth.

01

China Factory Sourcing

We identify and qualify manufacturers across Guangdong, Fujian, and Zhejiang for garment accessories, hardware, and leather. Factory audits and sample coordination included.

02

Export Documentation

Complete bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin handling for shipments via Xiamen, Amoy, and Hong Kong ports to destinations worldwide.

03

Bulk & Stock Wholesale

Stock holding in Hong Kong and Mainland China enables fast dispatch for repeat buyers. Minimum order quantities are flexible for both trial and bulk orders.

04

Custom Product Development

Private-label and custom specification products available for larger clients — including custom colour, finish, logo branding, and exclusive packaging for OEM/ODM requirements.

05

Quality Inspection

Pre-shipment quality checks performed at factory or third-party inspection centres to ensure goods meet buyer specifications before departure.

06

Freight Coordination

We work with trusted freight forwarders for FCL/LCL sea freight and air freight options — routing through Xiamen Port, Hong Kong, and Shanghai for cost-effective delivery.

Our Trade Routes

Fuxing Trade has established buyer relationships and shipping lanes connecting Hong Kong and South China to markets across the Americas, South Asia, and beyond.

🇺🇸

United States

Regular shipments via Xiamen Port to Seattle, WA — servicing wholesale distributors and garment accessory importers on the US West Coast. Bill of lading via OOLU and DFDS.

🇧🇩

Bangladesh

Supply of cow crust leather and hides to tanneries at Chattogram. Bangladesh's thriving garment and footwear manufacturing sector is a key buyer of our leather products.

🇪🇨

Ecuador

Export of automotive wheel bolts and hardware components to distributors in Ecuador — servicing the South American aftermarket auto parts replacement industry.

🇨🇳

China (Supply Base)

Sourcing hub in Xiamen and the Pearl River Delta region — factories supply garment accessories, hardware, and textiles for re-export through Hong Kong.

🇭🇰

Hong Kong Hub

Mong Kok office coordinates regional operations, trade documentation, client management, and logistics for seamless cross-border trading flows.

🌏

Emerging Markets

Actively expanding into Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Eastern Europe — seeking new buyer partnerships for our garment accessories and industrial product lines.
