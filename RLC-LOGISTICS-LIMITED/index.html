Comprehensive Logistics Solutions

RLC Logistics provides end-to-end freight and logistics services, leveraging Hong Kong's strategic position as Asia's premier logistics hub.

🚢
Sea Freight
Full Container Load (FCL) and Less than Container Load (LCL) ocean freight services covering major global ports, with full documentation and customs clearance support.
✈️
Air Freight
International airfreight forwarding for time-sensitive shipments. We work with major airlines to provide competitive rates and reliable transit times worldwide.
🚛
Land Transport
Local Hong Kong delivery and cross-border China trucking services. Flexible scheduling and reliable door-to-door delivery across the Greater Bay Area.
📋
Customs Clearance
Professional import and export customs declaration services. Experienced in Hong Kong and Mainland China customs regulations for smooth cargo clearance.
🏭
Warehousing & Distribution
Short and long-term warehousing, cargo consolidation, repacking and local distribution services to support your supply chain operations.
🔗
Supply Chain Solutions
Customised multimodal logistics solutions combining sea, air and land transport to optimise your supply chain cost and efficiency.
