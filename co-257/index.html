About Us

Quality Furniture &
Professional Engineering

優質傢俱 · 專業工程

AMAZE Furniture & Engineering Company Limited (海斯傢俱工程有限公司) is a Hong Kong-based supplier of quality commercial furniture and provider of comprehensive renovation engineering services.

Based in Yuen Long, we supply premium furniture solutions for schools, kindergartens, offices, restaurants, and commercial premises throughout Hong Kong, combining product excellence with full fit-out engineering capabilities.

Our product range encompasses office seating, school furniture, storage systems, soft seating, display solutions, and partition screens — supported by full renovation and engineering services to transform any space.
