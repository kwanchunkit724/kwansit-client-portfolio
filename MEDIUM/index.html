Our Story

Built in Hong Kong, Shaped by Its Times

Hong Kong in 1998 was a city in transformation. A year after the handover, at the peak of the Asian financial crisis, it was also a city that understood the power of information, clarity, and credible voices. That founding spirit — of honest, careful, context-driven media — shapes everything we do today.

Our Editorial Standards

What We Stand For

Six principles that guide every story, every article, and every word we publish or produce.

01 — ACCURACY
Facts First
Every claim is verified. We do not publish unconfirmed information. In an era of misinformation, getting it right matters more than getting it first.
02 — INDEPENDENCE
No Sacred Cows
Our editorial positions are independent of advertisers and commercial interests. We call it as we see it, even when it's inconvenient.
03 — DEPTH
Context Over Clicks
We resist the race to the bottom. Our readers deserve context, nuance, and analysis — not just headlines engineered to provoke a reaction.
04 — TRANSPARENCY
We Show Our Work
Sponsored content is labelled. Editorial is editorial. We never obscure the relationship between commerce and content from our readers.
05 — CRAFT
Writing That Endures
Journalism is a craft. We invest in the quality of our writing — structure, voice, precision, and the kind of prose that rewards careful reading.
06 — RELEVANCE
For Hong Kong, About Hong Kong
Everything we produce is grounded in the HK reality. We are not a wire service running global feeds — we are a local publisher that understands this city.
