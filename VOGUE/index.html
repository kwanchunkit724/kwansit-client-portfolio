About Hit The Floor

Hong Kong's Digital
Lifestyle Voice

Hit The Floor is a Hong Kong-based digital lifestyle and culture platform that captures the energy, creativity and diversity of the city. We cover everything from the hottest fashion moments and beauty trends to arts, culture, nightlife and the people shaping Hong Kong's vibrant scene.

Our editorial team blends global perspectives with deep local knowledge, creating content that resonates with Hong Kong's cosmopolitan audience — expats, locals and culture lovers who demand more from their media diet.
