Family Legacy

Three Generations of Craftsmanship

For nearly a century, the Yu family has guided Sunnex from a small Sham Shui Po workshop to a globally respected brand, with each generation building on the last's legacy.

1st
Mr. Yu Kan Hing
1929 – Founder, Sunbeam Mfg. Co., Ltd.
Founded Sunbeam Manufacturing in Sham Shui Po, Hong Kong, producing the famous "Lion" brand flashlights. His vision was to create employment for friends and neighbours from Guangdong, building a workforce of approximately 100 people.
2nd
Dr. Henry Yu
1972 – Founded Sunnex Products Limited
Transformed the business by pivoting to stainless steel products. Named "Sunnex" to honour the Sunbeam legacy (retaining "Sun") while signifying the next step forward ("nex"). Built the brand's iconic tea service range beloved by restaurants globally.
3rd
Mr. Michael Yu
2002 – China Sales Department Established
After thorough market research, established the China Sales Department in 2002, registering the Sunnex trademark for the mainland market and building nationwide distribution through South and North Sales Centres covering all provinces and major cities.
1989
Shenzhen Factory Established
Nanao, Shenzhen · 42,000 sqm
To capitalise on Mainland China's economic reforms, Sunnex moved its production base from Hong Kong to a purpose-built factory in Nanao, Shenzhen — a state-of-the-art facility totalling 42,000 square metres that continues to serve as the Group's manufacturing hub.
Development History

Nearly a Century of Innovation

Key milestones in Sunnex's journey from a small Hong Kong factory to an internationally distributed hospitality brand.

1929
Sunbeam Founded in Sham Shui Po
Mr. Yu Kan Hing establishes Sunbeam Mfg. Co., Ltd., producing "Lion" brand flashlights with ~100 workers.
1967
First Stainless Steel Order
The company's first stainless steel order — salt and pepper shaker moulds — leads Sunbeam to manufacture and export the shakers to the UK independently, launching a new chapter.
1972
Sunnex Products Limited Established
Dr. Henry Yu founds Sunnex Products Limited, specialising in stainless steel hollowware for the hospitality industry.
1970s–80s
Global Restaurant Success
Sunnex tea sets and the iconic Stackable Series (31300 & 21800) gain worldwide acceptance. Over 1 million teapots sold in the UK.
1989
China Factory Opens
42,000 sqm production facility established in Nanao, Shenzhen, as Sunnex embraces China's economic opening.
2002
China Market Expansion
Mr. Michael Yu establishes the China Sales Department, registering the Sunnex trademark and building nationwide distribution coverage.
2003
First Electric Soup Warmer
Launch of Sunnex's first electric soup warmer receives unprecedented global recognition, triggering development of a full range of electrical hospitality appliances.
2017
Burano Chafer — Breakthrough Innovation
Launch of the Burano Chafer, featuring precise ±1-2°C temperature control, 3-level presets, and an advanced hydraulic hinge rated for 80,000 cycles.
