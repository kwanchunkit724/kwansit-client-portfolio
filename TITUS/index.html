About TITUS

Independent, Modern, Results-Driven

TITUS is a modern independent law firm serving corporate clients, private companies, institutions, and individuals — with offices in Hong Kong, Japan, and Germany.

TITUS is an independent Hong Kong law firm that stands apart through its modern, technology-forward approach to legal services. Unlike traditional law firms, TITUS actively embraces technological innovation — keeping pace with the latest developments affecting local and global business, particularly in the areas of fintech, blockchain, and cryptocurrency law.

From its base in Admiralty's Lippo Centre, TITUS serves an international client base spanning individual entrepreneurs to large institutions, delivering efficient and results-driven legal solutions across 14 practice areas. Their alliance with Tokyo practice TA Law extends their reach into the Japanese market.
