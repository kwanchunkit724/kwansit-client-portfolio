Health Packages

Tailored Health Packages

OT&P offers a range of health packages designed to support you at every stage of life. From comprehensive body checks to corporate wellness programmes.

🌿
Longevity Packages
Comprehensive assessments focused on healthy ageing, metabolic health, and long-term vitality. Includes advanced screening and personalised wellness plans.
🤱
Pregnancy Packages
Complete antenatal care packages from first trimester through delivery — including scans, tests, midwifery consultations, and postnatal follow-up.
🔍
Body Check Packages
Comprehensive health screening covering blood work, imaging, cardiac assessment, and specialist consultation — tailored to your age and health history.
🌸
Wellness Packages
WellWorX holistic wellness programmes combining physical health, nutrition, mental wellbeing, and lifestyle coaching for optimal day-to-day health.
🏢
Corporate Packages
Customised employee health programmes for companies — from occupational health and pre-employment medicals to group vaccination and corporate wellness.
👶
Children Vaccine Packages
Structured childhood vaccination programmes following Hong Kong and international immunisation schedules — with paediatric consultation included.
