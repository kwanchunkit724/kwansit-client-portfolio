Quality Management System

ISO 9001:2015 Certified

AG Logistics (Hong Kong) Company Limited's Quality Management System meets the requirements of ISO 9001:2015 for freight forwarding and logistics services. We maintain established methods, processes, and performance metrics against defined objectives.

Environmental Management System

About AG Logistics

Your Trusted Logistics Partner

AG Logistics (Hong Kong) Company Limited is a professional international third-party logistics provider with a focus on delivering global end-to-end supply chain management. We work closely with clients to understand and customise solutions to their specific needs.

With our global network of reliable and experienced freight agents, ISO-certified quality systems, and Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) integration capabilities, we provide clients and partners with seamless data transmission and transparent logistics management.

Our Thailand affiliate, AG World Transport, holds AEO status — extending our commitment to secure, compliant supply chains into Southeast Asia.
