Our Story

Born in Hong Kong

Eugina Limited was founded with a simple belief: every woman deserves access to beautiful, well-made products at reasonable prices. Based in Hong Kong, we bridge the best of global fashion with the needs of the modern Asian lifestyle.

We're a boutique — which means we're selective, personal, and genuinely invested in helping you find pieces you'll love. Our team curates continuously, ensuring fresh arrivals and a collection that evolves with the seasons.

  • Handpicked fashion, accessories, and lifestyle products
  • Regular new arrivals across all categories
  • Personalised styling advice available
  • Corporate gifting and bulk orders welcome
  • Hong Kong registered business with dedicated customer service
