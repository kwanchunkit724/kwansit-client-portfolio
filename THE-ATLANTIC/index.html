About Us

Ideas, Debate,
and Honest Journalism

Founded in 1857 in Boston, The Atlantic is one of the oldest and most respected publications in the United States. For over 165 years we have championed rigorous journalism, intellectual debate, and the exploration of the ideas that shape our society.

The Atlantic publishes across digital, print, and live events — bringing together the most distinguished writers, thinkers, and journalists to examine the pressing questions of our time. From politics and science to culture, education, and international affairs, The Atlantic is where important conversations begin.

Editorial Coverage

What We Cover

The Atlantic covers the ideas, politics, and cultural forces shaping our world — with depth, independence, and an unwillingness to take the easy answer.

🏛️
Politics
Rigorous, nonpartisan political analysis covering American democracy, governance, elections, and the evolving relationship between citizens and their institutions.
🔬
Science & Technology
Reporting on the scientific discoveries, technological transformations, and ethical dilemmas reshaping how we live, work, and understand the world.
🎭
Culture
Criticism and analysis of literature, film, music, art, and the broader cultural currents that define — and challenge — our shared values and identities.
🌍
International Affairs
Thoughtful coverage of global politics, international conflicts, diplomacy, and the forces reshaping the world order and America's role within it.
🧠
Ideas & Essays
Long-form essays and intellectual debates on philosophy, ethics, education, economics, and the ideas that define our moment and challenge our assumptions.
❤️
Health & Family
Evidence-based reporting on mental and physical health, education, parenting, relationships, and the science of human wellbeing and happiness.
