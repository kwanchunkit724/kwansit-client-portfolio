About Autobahn88 Group

Leading Hong Kong Performance Parts Manufacturer

Autobahn88 Group Limited is a leading supplier of intercoolers, front mount intercooler piping kits, oil cooler kits, racing radiators, stainless steel exhaust systems, and silicone hose — serving global distributors from Hong Kong.

Established in 2000 and based in Hong Kong, Autobahn88 Group Limited has grown into one of the region's leading manufacturers and wholesale suppliers of performance car parts and accessories. The company operates through four specialist production teams, each dedicated to a specific category of performance components.

Autobahn88 serves first-tier and second-tier markets across the Americas, Europe, and beyond — supplying performance enthusiasts, aftermarket distributors, resellers, and retailers with high-quality parts that compete with the world's leading brands.

The company actively seeks European distributors, traders, and resellers who specialise in performance parts and car accessories, whether through retail stores or online platforms.
