Print Services

Two Core Print Services

Whether you need 500 copies or 500,000, Regal Printing has the right technology and the right price

🏭
Large Volume Offset Printing

High-volume commercial offset printing in Hong Kong and China — the most cost-effective solution for large print runs of books, catalogues, calendars, and paper products. Precision CMYK colour reproduction, wide substrate range, and full finishing options including softcover, hardcover, Wire-O, and saddle stitch binding.

High Volume CMYK Offset HK & China Production Cost Effective All Binding Types
🖥️
Print on Demand (Digital)

Short-run digital Print on Demand services for publishers, self-publishers, and businesses needing small quantities — from one copy upwards. No minimum order, fast turnaround, and the same quality standards as our offset production. Perfect for test runs, personalised editions, and low-inventory publishing models.

How It Works

From Files to Finished Books

1
Quotation

2
File Review

Submit print-ready files. Our pre-press team checks all files before production commences.

3
