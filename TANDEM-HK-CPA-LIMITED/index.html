TANDEM (HK) CPA Limited is a Hong Kong-registered certified public accounting firm, providing professional audit, accounting, tax, and business advisory services to companies across various industries.

The name "Tandem" reflects our core philosophy — we work alongside our clients, in true partnership, moving forward together toward shared goals. We don't just file your accounts; we genuinely invest in understanding your business and supporting its growth.

Our experienced team of CPAs brings deep knowledge of Hong Kong accounting standards, tax law, and regulatory requirements, delivering reliable, accurate, and timely services that give you confidence and peace of mind.
