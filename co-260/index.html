About Us

Quality & Integrity
in Every Project

誠信為本 · 品質卓越

Established in 2009, Housewell (HK) Construction Limited (恆安(香港)工程有限公司) has been dedicated to delivering outstanding building and renovation services throughout Hong Kong. With over 1,200 completed projects spanning residential, commercial, and institutional premises, we bring expertise and precision to every engagement.

Our services cover the full spectrum of building works — from structural surveys and inspections through to interior design, renovation, electrical and mechanical installations, and fire safety systems. Our goal in every project is to achieve the ideal result for our clients.

Government Licensing

Fully Licensed & Certified

政府認可持牌承辦商

Housewell (HK) Construction holds official licences from the Hong Kong Government — ensuring all works are legally compliant, professionally executed, and covered by appropriate insurance.

HK Government
Small Works Contractor
小型工程承辦商
Registered with the Buildings Department of Hong Kong as a licensed Small Works Contractor. Authorised to carry out a range of prescribed minor works and alterations for residential and commercial buildings — ensuring full legal compliance for every project.
HK Government
Electrical Contractor
電業承辦商
Licenced Electrical Contractor registered under the Electricity Ordinance (Cap. 406) of Hong Kong. Our qualified electrical workers carry out all electrical works in full compliance with the Code of Practice for the Electricity (Wiring) Regulations.
