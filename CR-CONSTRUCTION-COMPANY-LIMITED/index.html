About Us

Who We Are

CR Construction Company Limited is a Hong Kong–listed construction enterprise with a proven track record in both new building construction and RMAA works. As a publicly listed company on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, we are held to the highest standards of corporate governance, transparency, and accountability.

Our dedicated teams of engineers, project managers, and skilled tradespeople work in concert to deliver projects on schedule, within budget, and to the exacting quality standards our clients expect. Our safety culture is embedded at every level of the organisation.

CR Construction's strength lies in our people — an experienced, multi-disciplinary workforce committed to continuous professional development, innovation in construction methods, and sustainable building practices that contribute to Hong Kong's long-term urban resilience.
