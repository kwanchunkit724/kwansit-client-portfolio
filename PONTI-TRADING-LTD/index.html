Our Heritage

35+ Years of Growth

From a single wine cellar in Kowloon to a multi-market group serving Asia's finest establishments.

1988

The Beginning

Ponti Food & Wine Cellar Ltd. established at the basement of Shui Hing Department Store, Kowloon — bringing fine wines to Hong Kong.

1994

Wholesale Focus

Ponti Trading Ltd. set up to focus on wine and food wholesale distribution, supplying hotels, clubhouses, restaurants and retailers.

1997

Singapore Expansion

Excaliber Fine Wines (S) Pte Ltd. opened in Singapore, responding to the city-state's growing wine market and appetite for fine dining.

2003

Premium Retail

Ponti Wine Cellars Pte Ltd. established as a premium wine specialist in Singapore, catering to the private collectors and connoisseurs market.

2019

Natural & Organic

Natural Ponti Ltd. launched in Hong Kong, dedicated to the fast-growing health and organic food sector, diversifying the group's product portfolio.

Today

Asia's Premier Distributor

Five companies across three markets — Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore — standing as one of Asia's leading wine and gourmet food distributors.
