About Haymarket Asia

Asia Pacific's Trusted B2B Media Group

Haymarket Media Group has been serving Asia Pacific's business professionals for decades through industry-defining editorial, intelligence, and events across marketing, advertising, and communications.

🌏 Part of Haymarket Media Group

Haymarket Media Group, founded in the UK, is one of the world's leading business media companies. In Asia Pacific, Haymarket operates from its Hong Kong base, publishing premier brands including Campaign Asia-Pacific — the definitive authority on marketing and advertising across the region — reaching senior marketing, agency, and media professionals across 25+ markets.

Our editorial teams combine deep regional expertise with global perspectives, delivering news, analysis, data, and intelligence that marketing and communications professionals rely on to make better decisions and drive business growth.

Beyond publishing, Haymarket organises the most prestigious industry events in Asia Pacific — from the annual Agency of the Year Awards to thought leadership conferences — creating platforms for the industry's leading voices.
