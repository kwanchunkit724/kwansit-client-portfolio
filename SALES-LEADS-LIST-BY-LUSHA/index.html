Production Capabilities

State-of-the-art equipment and proven expertise delivering consistent, high-quality results

🎨

Colour Management

ISO-calibrated colour management workflow ensuring accurate CMYK and Pantone reproduction across all print runs. Colour proofing available before final production.

Pre-Press & Design Support

In-house pre-press team checking files for print-readiness — resolution, bleed, colour mode, and font embedding — minimising errors before press.

Express Production

Same-day and next-day print options for urgent requirements. Our digital presses can turn around short runs within hours for business-critical deadlines.

Eco-Friendly Options

FSC-certified paper stocks, vegetable-based inks, and water-based laminates available for clients prioritising sustainable and environmentally responsible print production.

Fulfilment & Delivery

In-house collating, packing, and delivery across Hong Kong. We also offer direct mail fulfilment — print, personalise, address, and post in one seamless workflow.

Quality Control

Every job inspected before despatch. Strict QC protocols covering colour accuracy, registration, cut tolerance, and finish quality to our exacting house standards.
