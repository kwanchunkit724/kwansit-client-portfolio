About Withers

A Different Kind of International Law Firm

Withers is internationally recognised as a leader in serving ultra-high-net-worth individuals, family offices, private businesses, and entrepreneurs across the globe.

Founded in London in 1896, Withers has grown into one of the world's most distinctive international law firms. Unlike firms focused primarily on corporate clients, Withers has built its global reputation on its exceptional private client and wealth management practice — serving the world's most successful individuals, families, and their businesses.

The Hong Kong office, registered as Withers HK Professional Services Limited, is a key hub in the firm's Asia-Pacific presence. The office serves clients in Hong Kong, Greater China, and across the Asia-Pacific region, providing expertise in both private client matters and complex commercial transactions.

Withers Hong Kong combines deep local market knowledge with the firm's global network, enabling seamless cross-border legal solutions for clients with international needs.
