About The HK Hub

Your Trusted Guide
to Life in Hong Kong

The HK Hub was created by Hong Kong residents who wanted to build the most reliable, up-to-date resource for exploring everything this incredible city has to offer. Whether you're a new expat navigating the city, a long-time resident looking for hidden gems, or a visitor planning the perfect trip, The HK Hub has you covered.

Our team of local experts and contributors personally researches and reviews each recommendation, ensuring you get honest, practical advice — not just a recycled listicle. Every guide is regularly updated to reflect the latest openings, closures, and changes in the city.

  • Expert, locally-researched "Best of Hong Kong" guides across all lifestyle categories
  • Regularly updated content reflecting 2026 recommendations and new openings
  • Covers beauty, dining, wellness, travel, activities, shopping and more
  • Targeted content for expats, locals and visitors to Hong Kong
  • Advertising and partnership opportunities for Hong Kong businesses

🎯 Our Mission

To be the most trusted, comprehensive and up-to-date digital guide to living and exploring in Hong Kong — connecting residents, expats and visitors with the very best the city has to offer, from world-class nail salons to hidden rooftop bars.
