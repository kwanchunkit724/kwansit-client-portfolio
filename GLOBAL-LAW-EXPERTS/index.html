About Global Law Experts

The World's Premier Legal Expert Matching Platform

Global Law Experts (GLE) is an international legal directory and matching platform that connects clients with vetted legal experts in jurisdictions around the world.

Global Law Experts connects businesses, investors, and high-net-worth families with trusted, vetted lawyers in more than 140 countries. Our platform serves clients who need reliable legal expertise in jurisdictions where they have no existing professional relationships.

Our rigorous vetting process ensures that every law firm listed on our platform meets high standards of professional competence and client service. When you submit a request, our matching system identifies the best-fit legal experts for your specific needs — combining jurisdiction, practice area, and client type.

GLE also offers law firms digital marketing and lead generation services to help them reach international clients seeking legal expertise in their jurisdiction.
