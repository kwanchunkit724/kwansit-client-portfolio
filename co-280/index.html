About Us

Hong Kong's Foundation
Construction Specialist

現恆建築控股有限公司

In Construction Holdings Limited (現恆建築控股有限公司, Stock Code: 1500) is an investment holding company whose principal subsidiary, In Construction Limited (現恆建築有限公司), operates as a specialist contractor in the foundation and civil engineering sector in Hong Kong.

Founded in 1996 and headquartered in Causeway Bay, the company has built a strong track record across Hong Kong's demanding construction landscape — undertaking foundation, site formation, demolition, ground investigation, and general building works for major residential, commercial, and public sector developments.

As a listed entity on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, the Group adheres to the highest standards of corporate governance, transparency, and professional service delivery — supported by experienced management and a skilled technical workforce.

Our Services

Foundation & Construction Services

地基及建築工程服務

Specialist services covering the full spectrum of foundation, geotechnical, demolition, and building works for major development projects across Hong Kong.

⚒️
Foundation Works
地基工程
Comprehensive piling solutions including bored piles, driven steel H-piles, minipiles, pre-bored socketted H-piles, pipe piles, and raft footing foundations for all types of development.
🏔️
Site Formation
土地平整
Site formation works including slope stabilisation, soil nails, temporary working platforms, and ELS (Excavation & Lateral Support) systems for challenging terrain and level-difference sites.
💣
Demolition Works
拆卸工程
Professional demolition of existing structures with full compliance to statutory requirements, site safety management, and environmental protection measures throughout the process.
🔩
ELS & Retaining Works
臨時支撐及擋土工程
Design and construction of Excavation & Lateral Support (ELS) systems, sheet pile walls, soldier pile walls, minipile walls, and permanent retaining walls for deep excavation projects.
🔬
Ground Investigation
地質勘探
Field ground investigation services including borehole drilling, soil sampling, in-situ testing, and geotechnical assessment to support foundation design and construction planning.
🏗️
General Building Works
一般建築工程
General building, superstructure, and civil engineering works encompassing structural concrete, pile cap construction, superstructure to podium level, and vehicular access ramp construction.

Piling & Foundation Methods

樁基工程技術

Our engineering team is experienced across the full range of piling and foundation methods used in Hong Kong's complex urban construction environment.

🔩
Bored Piles
鑽孔樁
Standard and large diameter bored pile installation for heavy structural loads.
⚙️
Driven H-Piles
打入型鋼樁
Steel H-pile driving for sites requiring high load capacity in restricted access conditions.
🏗️
Minipiles
微型樁
Small-diameter minipiles ideal for low headroom, restricted access or underpinning works.
🔲
Sheet Piles & ELS

Temporary and permanent earth retention using sheet piles, soldier piles, and lateral support systems.

Soil Nails

Ground stabilisation using soil nail systems for slope protection and cut face retention.
