About Kin Yu

Engineering Excellence
Since 2014

Kin Yu Engineering HK Company Limited is a specialist engineering company involved in both consultancy and construction works. Founded by Mr Chan and Dr Lee, whose combined expertise spans more than 15 years in industry, consulting, and research.

The company's diverse project portfolio reflects its capability across building construction and consultancy works. Kin Yu's core project portfolio focuses on slope stabilisation and drainage works for proposed residential and commercial developments in Hong Kong, in partnership with Elite Trading and Engineering Company Limited and Po Hsing Construction Company Limited.

Beyond builder works, Kin Yu provides consultancy services including ELS (Earth-retaining and Lateral-support) design and slope stability analyses — offering clients a genuine end-to-end engineering solution.
