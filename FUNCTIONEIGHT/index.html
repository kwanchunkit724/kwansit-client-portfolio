About FunctionEight

20+ Years Empowering Businesses with Technology

From Wan Chai to Singapore, FunctionEight has been the trusted IT backbone for hundreds of organisations across Asia since 2001.

FunctionEight is a premier Managed IT Services provider operating across Asia, with a 200-strong team of IT professionals dedicated to keeping your business running at peak performance — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Since our founding in 2001, we have built deep expertise in enterprise IT support, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity — serving clients from SMEs to multinational corporations across Hong Kong, Singapore, and beyond.

Our commitment to low staff turnover means your account is handled by engineers who know your systems inside out, delivering consistent, reliable service year after year.
