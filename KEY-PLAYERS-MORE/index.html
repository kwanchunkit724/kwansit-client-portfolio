Services

What We Do

From operational consulting to executive recruitment, we bring specialist expertise to every engagement — helping logistics businesses grow, compete, and perform at their best.

📊

Logistics Consulting

Process review, network design, and operational improvement for freight forwarders, 3PLs, and shipper logistics teams. We identify inefficiencies and deliver actionable improvement plans.

🔍

Executive Search

Specialist recruitment for senior and mid-level roles across logistics, supply chain, shipping, and trade finance — connecting companies with the key players that drive growth.

🌐

Market Entry Advisory

Strategic guidance for logistics companies entering new Asian markets — covering regulatory requirements, partner identification, local operations setup, and go-to-market strategy.

🤝

Business Development

Sales strategy, channel partner identification, and key account management support for logistics service providers looking to grow their client base in Hong Kong and across Asia.

📋

Compliance & Trade Advisory

Guidance on trade compliance, import/export regulations, and customs procedures across key Asian markets — helping businesses stay compliant as regulations evolve.

🏋️

Training & Development

Tailored training programmes for logistics teams — covering freight operations, customer service, trade documentation, and leadership development for emerging managers.

Talent Solutions

Finding the Right People

Great logistics businesses are built on great people. Our talent solutions connect you with experienced professionals across the full supply chain spectrum.

🎯

Executive & Senior Leadership

C-suite and VP-level placements for logistics companies — from Country Managers and Operations Directors to Chief Supply Chain Officers. Confidential searches handled with discretion.

👤

Mid-Level Specialists

Experienced freight operations managers, trade compliance officers, business development managers, and logistics analysts placed with leading companies across Hong Kong and Asia.

🌏

Cross-Border Talent

Bilingual (Cantonese/Mandarin/English) logistics professionals for Greater China operations — ideal for companies bridging Hong Kong and mainland China supply chains.

📈

Contract & Interim Roles

Short-term and project-based logistics professionals to cover peak periods, system implementations, or strategic projects — ready to deliver from day one.
