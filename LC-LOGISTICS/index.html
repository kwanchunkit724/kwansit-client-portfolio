Services

End-to-End Logistics Solutions

From vessel ownership and chartering to freight forwarding and supply chain management, LC Logistics provides comprehensive maritime and logistics services to global clients.

🚢

Container Shipping

Full container load (FCL) and less than container load (LCL) shipping services across major global trade lanes, leveraging a network of carrier partnerships and owned vessel capacity.

⚓

Vessel Chartering & Management

Time charter and voyage charter arrangements for containerships up to 14,000 TEU. Expert vessel acquisition, resale, and fleet management services for shipping investors and operators.

📦

Freight Forwarding

Comprehensive sea freight forwarding services including booking, documentation, customs brokerage, and cargo insurance — covering import, export, and cross-trade shipments worldwide.

🏭

Warehousing & Distribution

Hong Kong and regional warehousing solutions with inventory management, pick-and-pack, and last-mile distribution services to support e-commerce and B2B supply chains.

🔗

Supply Chain Management

End-to-end supply chain visibility and optimisation — from factory in Asia to distribution centre worldwide. Integrated systems for real-time cargo tracking and reporting.

📋

Customs & Trade Compliance

Expert handling of import/export customs declarations, trade compliance advisory, and documentation services to facilitate smooth cross-border cargo movements.
