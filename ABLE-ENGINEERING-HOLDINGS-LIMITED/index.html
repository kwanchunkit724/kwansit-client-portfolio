Our History

Company Milestones

From a small engineering company in 1976 to a HKEX-listed construction group — Able Engineering's journey reflects the growth of Hong Kong's built environment.

1976

Established as Yeu Can Engineering Co., Ltd — laying the foundation for decades of construction excellence in Hong Kong.

1999

Built strong industry reputation. Appointed by the Hong Kong Institution of Engineers to provide Scheme "A" Graduate Training to engineering graduates.

2009+

Expanded project portfolio under Architectural Services Department — General Outpatient Clinics, Community Mental Health Centres, and Long Stay Care Homes.

2016

Received ArchSD Green Contractor Bronze Award — recognising our commitment to green construction practices and sustainable building.

2017

Listed on the Main Board of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (Stock Code: 1627) on 20 February 2017 — a milestone reflecting our growth and transparency.

Now

Continuing to build landmark projects across Hong Kong, with a commitment to exceeding expectations on every project delivered.
