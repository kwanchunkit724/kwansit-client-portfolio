Our Services

Complete Container Solutions

EW delivers six core services spanning the full container lifecycle — from purchase and leasing to maintenance, repositioning, and shipping.

📦

Container Sales

New and used containers including Dry, Reefer, Open Side, and other special container types. Competitive pricing sourced from our global depot network.

🔄

One Way Leasing

Cost-effective worldwide one-way leasing through our global partner network in China, Southeast Asia, India, Australia, Europe, the UK, and the United States.

🔧

Container Spare Parts & Components

Supply of container parts and accessories for maintenance and repair. Supporting fleet owners and operators to keep their containers in prime condition.

🌍

Empty Repositioning

Efficient empty container repositioning services for standard and special containers worldwide. Optimise your fleet placement and reduce imbalance costs.

🏭

Depot Services

Repair, maintenance, and storage services at depot locations including Hong Kong, China, Vietnam, and Thailand. Full container care in key Asian hubs.

🚢

Ship | NVOCC Agencies

Shipping services from Shanghai, Qingdao, and Shenzhen to India with weekly regular spaces. Operating new routes including the Red Sea corridor.
