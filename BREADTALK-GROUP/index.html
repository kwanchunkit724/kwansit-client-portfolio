Our Story

From One Bakery to a Global Culinary Empire

BreadTalk Group began with a bold vision — to transform bread from a functional staple into a cultural lifestyle experience. Today, we are one of Asia's most recognised F&B groups.

Established in 2000 in Singapore, BreadTalk Group was founded with a passion for creativity and product innovation. What began as a single bakery has evolved into a global culinary enterprise spanning 14 international markets with over 700 outlets and six iconic consumer brands.

BreadTalk Group manages a diverse portfolio spanning bakeries, food courts, premium restaurants and innovative food concepts. The group's brands — including the flagship BreadTalk bakery chain and the Din Tai Fung franchise — have become household names across Asia and beyond.

With a team of 5,200+ passionate employees and deep operational expertise in bakery, food atrium management, and restaurant operations, BreadTalk Group continues to expand its global footprint while staying true to its founding mission of culinary creativity and customer delight.

Brand Portfolio

Iconic Brands, Countless Experiences

Six flagship brands spanning bakery, café, food court, restaurant and innovative concepts — each with a distinct identity and devoted following.

🍞
BreadTalk
The Original Artisan Bakery
BreadTalk's flagship bakery chain pioneered the concept of open-kitchen display bakeries in Asia — turning bread into a lifestyle experience. With 500+ locations across 14 markets, it remains the group's core and most recognisable brand.
500+ Bakeries Globally
☕
Toast Box
Traditional Kopi Culture
Celebrating the traditional Nanyang coffeehouse culture, Toast Box brings authentic kaya toast, soft-boiled eggs, and Nanyang-style coffee to modern café settings across Singapore and the region.
Singapore & Regional
🍜
Din Tai Fung
World-Renowned Dumpling Restaurant
BreadTalk Group holds the franchise rights for Din Tai Fung — the iconic Taiwanese restaurant famed for its xiao long bao — across Singapore, Thailand and the United Kingdom, including locations in London.
Franchise: SG · TH · UK
🏪
Food Republic
Premium Food Atrium
An upscale food hall concept offering a curated selection of local and international hawker-inspired cuisine across 40+ outlets in Singapore, Malaysia and Greater China — blending heritage flavours with modern ambiance.
40+ Outlets · SG · MY · CN
🍱
Food Junction
Neighbourhood Food Court
BreadTalk's community food court brand, operating 9 outlets across Singapore and Malaysia, providing accessible, affordable and diverse meal options for everyday dining needs.
9 Outlets · SG · MY
🥣
Thye Moh Chan
Heritage Teochew Pastry
A venerable heritage brand celebrated for its traditional Teochew-style pastries and mooncakes. Thye Moh Chan preserves Singapore's baking heritage while bringing timeless confections to new generations.
Singapore Heritage Brand
