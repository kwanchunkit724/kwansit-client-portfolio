Who We Are

A Trusted Name in Hong Kong Trade

Lexington Limited is a Hong Kong-based international trading company specialising in the procurement and distribution of furniture, kitchenware, and tableware. We connect quality global manufacturers with buyers across the Asian market.

With our office in Sai Wan Ho, Hong Kong Island, we are strategically positioned at the heart of one of the world's most dynamic trading hubs, enabling efficient transactions with partners across the globe.

  • International purchasing expertise across furniture, kitchen, and lifestyle categories
  • Established relationships with suppliers across Europe, Asia, and North America
  • Quality verification and compliance checks on all products
  • Efficient logistics coordination from source to destination
  • Competitive pricing through volume purchasing and supplier relationships
  • Personalised service with direct access to our experienced team
