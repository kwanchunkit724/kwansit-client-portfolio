About Guoco Group

A Diversified International Conglomerate

Guoco Group Limited is a Hong Kong-listed investment holding company with diversified business interests spanning multiple sectors and geographies across Asia and internationally.

Guoco Group Limited is a member of the Hong Leong Group (Malaysia), one of Southeast Asia's most established conglomerates. With a portfolio of publicly listed subsidiaries and strategic investments, Guoco Group operates across property development, hospitality, gaming and leisure, and financial services.

Listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange under stock code 53, Guoco Group has built a reputation for identifying and growing businesses with strong long-term fundamentals across Asia and major international markets.

The group's strategy focuses on creating shareholder value through disciplined capital allocation, active portfolio management, and the development of landmark property and hospitality assets across Singapore, Malaysia, China, and internationally.
