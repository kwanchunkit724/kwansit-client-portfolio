✦ Trusted by Hong Kong's Corporate Leaders

Since 2014, Bingo Time Catering has been the go-to choice for Hong Kong's most prestigious corporate events. As a designated supplier for HKEX listing ceremonies since 2015, we have been privileged to celebrate milestones with over 1,300 companies on their most important days.

Our philosophy is simple: every event, large or small, deserves exceptional food, gracious service, and meticulous coordination. Whether it's a landmark stock exchange listing attended by hundreds, or an intimate birthday gathering, we bring the same level of passion and professionalism.

Located in Kwun Tong, our dedicated team of culinary professionals and event coordinators work hand-in-hand to craft customized menus that reflect your vision, budget, and style — ensuring every guest leaves impressed.

Signature Expertise

🏛️ Hong Kong Stock Exchange Listing Ceremonies

Since 2015, Bingo Time Catering has been a designated catering supplier for HKEX listing ceremonies — one of Hong Kong's most prestigious corporate milestones. We have proudly served the celebrations of 1,300+ companies as they rang the bell on their historic listing days, and successfully catered landmark events including the Shenzhen-Hong Kong and Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect opening ceremonies for over 800 guests.

Our HKEX listing ceremony catering service is designed to be seamless, elegant, and perfectly timed — ensuring every executive, investor, and guest enjoys a world-class experience on this momentous occasion.
