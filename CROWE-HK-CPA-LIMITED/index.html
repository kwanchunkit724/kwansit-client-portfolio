OUR SERVICES

Professional Accounting & Advisory Services

As a member of Crowe Global, Crowe (HK) offers the depth of a global network combined with local expertise, providing integrated professional services across audit, tax, advisory and consulting.

📊
Audit & Assurance
Statutory and voluntary audits conducted in accordance with Hong Kong Standards on Auditing, delivering independent assurance to boards, management and stakeholders on financial reporting quality.
📝
Tax Services
Comprehensive Hong Kong and cross-border tax advisory, compliance and planning services, including profits tax, salaries tax, stamp duty and international tax structuring for multinational operations.
💡
Advisory Services
Transaction advisory, due diligence, valuation and mergers & acquisitions support. We help businesses navigate complex transactions with confidence and clarity backed by global expertise.
🛡️
Risk & Compliance
Enterprise risk management, internal audit, internal controls review and regulatory compliance advisory to strengthen governance frameworks and protect organisational value.
💼
Business Consulting
Finance transformation, digital strategy, process improvement and technology advisory helping organisations optimise performance, reduce costs and build resilience in an evolving business environment.
🏢
Corporate Services
Company incorporation, company secretarial, accounting and payroll services for businesses establishing or expanding operations in Hong Kong, supported by the Crowe Global network across Asia.
