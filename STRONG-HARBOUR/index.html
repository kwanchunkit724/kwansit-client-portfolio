About Strong Harbour

Trusted Food & Beverage Wholesale Distributor

Strong Harbour (昌港有限公司) is a Hong Kong-based trading and wholesale distribution company specialising in food and beverage products across nine major categories.

Strong Harbour 昌港有限公司 is an established food and beverage wholesale distributor based in Yau Tong, Hong Kong. The company trades and distributes a comprehensive range of food products — from fresh produce including vegetables, mushrooms, and fruit, to meat, cooking ingredients, desserts, wine, and beverages.

With a portfolio of trusted brands and established distribution channels, Strong Harbour serves wholesale buyers and the broader food and beverage trade throughout Hong Kong. The company offers both English and Chinese language services, facilitating access for local and international buyers alike.

Strong Harbour's nine major food categories provide buyers with a structured and comprehensive product catalogue, making it a convenient one-stop source for diverse F&B wholesale requirements.
