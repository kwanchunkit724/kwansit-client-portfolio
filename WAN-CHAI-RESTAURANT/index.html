About

Mediterranean Fare
in Wan Chai

"Enjoy expertly crafted dishes, attentive service, and an inviting atmosphere from one of the best restaurants in Wan Chai."

Welcome to The Drawing Room — the signature restaurant of The St. Regis Hong Kong. Perched on the second floor of this iconic luxury hotel at 1 Harbour Drive, Wan Chai, The Drawing Room offers a fresh and seasonal Mediterranean menu served throughout the day.

From a full breakfast spread to leisurely lunches and elegant dinners, the kitchen celebrates the vibrant flavours of the Mediterranean — bold olive oils, sun-ripened produce, fresh seafood and herb-infused preparations that evoke the spirit of the region.

In the afternoons, the atmosphere transforms entirely as The Drawing Room's celebrated Signature Ritual — a reimagined Afternoon Tea served by dim sum-inspired trolley — takes centre stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good to Know

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are encouraged, but not required. For groups or special occasions, booking ahead is strongly recommended.

Is there a children's menu?

Yes — The Drawing Room offers an expansive children's menu, making it a welcoming destination for family dining.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Sunday from 7:00 AM to 12:00 AM. Last food order is at 9:15 PM.

What cuisine do you serve?

The Drawing Room uses seasonal ingredients to prepare fresh and vibrant Mediterranean cuisine throughout the day.

How do I book for the Afternoon Tea?

The Signature Ritual Afternoon Tea is served daily from 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM. Book via phone (+852 2138 6800) or email.

Is private dining available?

The Drawing Room does not offer private dining rooms. For group or event bookings, please contact the team directly.
