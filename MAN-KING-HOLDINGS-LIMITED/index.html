Construction Services

Man King Holdings delivers a comprehensive suite of construction services across the public and private sectors in Hong Kong, supported by experienced professionals and strong subcontractor networks.

🏗️

Building Construction

New build construction for residential, commercial, institutional, and industrial developments. Full project management from tender to handover.

🔨

Repair, Maintenance & Renovation

RMAA works for existing buildings across all sectors. Planned and reactive maintenance, alterations, and additions to maximise asset value.

🪑

Fitting Out & Interior Works

Comprehensive interior fitting out services for offices, retail, hospitality, and institutional spaces. Design and build capability for turnkey delivery.

Government & Institutional Works

Experienced in government facility construction including schools, clinics, community centres, and public buildings under Architectural Services Department guidelines.

Residential Development

Private and public residential construction — from individual units to large housing estate developments in partnership with major Hong Kong developers.

M&E & Specialist Works
