About Marche

Printing Excellence
Built on Precision

Marche Label & Printing Ltd. (萬智商標印刷有限公司) is a Hong Kong-based award-winning label and printing company operating its own production facility in Kwun Tong. Founded on the principles of Persistence, Perseverance and Self-demand, Marche delivers premium printing solutions to some of Hong Kong's most prestigious brands.

🏆 Our Commitment to Excellence

"Our relentless persistence in completing every task with high quality drives our insatiable pursuit for perfection. And we deliver." — This is the Marche promise, backed by multiple Hong Kong Print Awards and the Smart Design Award 2021 Gold.

With multiple professional certifications and environmental safety accreditations, Marche serves leading clients in financial, hospitality, retail and catering sectors, providing tailored printing solutions from the company's own production workshop in Kwun Tong.

Award-Winning Quality

Multiple HK Print Awards (Gold, Champion, Bronze) and Smart Design Awards 2021 Gold.

Certified & Compliant

Multiple professional qualifications and environmental safety certifications ensuring quality assurance.

Own Production Facility

Full in-house production workshop in Kwun Tong for complete quality control and faster turnaround.
