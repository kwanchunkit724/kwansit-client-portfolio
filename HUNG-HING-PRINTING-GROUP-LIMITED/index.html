Our Story

75 Years of Printing Excellence

From humble beginnings in Hong Kong to a world-class printing platform — a legacy built on craftsmanship and innovation.

1950

Founded in Hong Kong

Hung Hing begins as a small printing house in Hong Kong, serving local businesses with quality offset printing.

1980s

Expansion into Book Printing

Growing reputation attracts international publishers, establishing Hung Hing as a trusted book printing partner for global markets.

1990s

Mainland China Operations

Strategic expansion into mainland China with multiple manufacturing plants, significantly scaling production capacity.

2000s

Asia's Leading Printing Platform

Grows to become one of Asia's major global players in books, packaging, paper trading, and promotional products.

2019

Vietnam Plant Established

Opening of Vietnam manufacturing facility extends Southeast Asia presence and diversifies production capabilities.

Today

US$281M Global Enterprise

5,000 employees, 7 plants, 12 warehouses, and US$281M in annual revenues — a true heritage of making ideas matter.
