About Carson

Trusted Since 1993

Carson Logistics (HK) Ltd. was founded in 1993 with a specialization in Chinese Mainland–Hong Kong logistics services. For over 30 years, we have been a trusted partner for businesses requiring reliable, efficient cross-border freight solutions between mainland China and Hong Kong.

Our operations are strategically located in Lau Fau Shan, New Territories — in close proximity to the China–HK border, giving us a significant operational advantage for cross-border trucking and logistics coordination. We provide one-stop service for import and export from mainland China to foreign countries through Hong Kong.

Our core business includes China–HK trucks, Hong Kong warehouse management, bulk cargo consolidation and distribution — backed by decades of hands-on experience and deep knowledge of customs and cross-border operations.
