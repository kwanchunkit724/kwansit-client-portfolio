About Us

Your Trusted Automotive Service Partner

Auto Repair and Service Group Limited provides professional automotive repair and maintenance services for a wide range of vehicle makes and models across Hong Kong.

Auto Repair and Service Group Limited is a professional automotive service group serving Hong Kong's vehicle owners with comprehensive repair and maintenance solutions. Our team of experienced technicians is equipped with the latest diagnostic tools to accurately identify and resolve issues across all makes and models.

We are committed to providing transparent, quality service at competitive prices — keeping your vehicle in optimal condition whether for routine maintenance or complex mechanical repairs. Our group operates with full accountability and a customer-first approach to automotive care.

From scheduled servicing and MOT preparation to accident repair and performance upgrades, Auto Repair and Service Group Limited is your one-stop partner for all automotive needs in Hong Kong.

Our Services

Comprehensive Automotive Services

From routine maintenance to complex repairs, our service group covers all aspects of vehicle care for both passenger cars and commercial vehicles.

🔧

Regular Maintenance & Servicing

Scheduled oil changes, filter replacements, fluid checks, brake inspections, and full vehicle service to manufacturer specifications — keeping your car running at peak efficiency.

💻

Diagnostic Services

Computerised engine diagnostic scanning, fault code reading and clearing, electrical system diagnosis, and in-depth analysis of warning lights and performance issues.

🛑

Brake & Suspension

Brake pad and disc replacement, brake fluid flushing, shock absorber inspection and replacement, wheel alignment, and steering system repairs.

⚙️

Engine & Transmission

Engine repairs and overhauls, timing belt/chain replacement, transmission service, gearbox repairs, and drivetrain maintenance for both manual and automatic vehicles.

🌡️

Cooling & Air Conditioning

Radiator repair and replacement, coolant flush and refill, AC system recharging, compressor repair, and full climate control system diagnostics.

⚡

Electrical Systems

Battery testing and replacement, alternator and starter motor repair, wiring diagnostics, lighting repairs, and electronic control unit programming and updates.
