About Us

Deppon in Hong Kong

Registered in Hong Kong since 2013, Deppon Logistics (HongKong) Limited serves as the gateway between Hong Kong's international trade hub and the vast Deppon Express network across Mainland China.

🏢 Part of Deppon Express Group

Deppon Express (德邦快递, SHA: 603056) was founded in 1996 and is now a core member of JD Logistics. The group is renowned for large-item express delivery (大件快递) and zero-load freight, operating one of China's most comprehensive logistics networks with over 30,000 service outlets and coverage across 1,700+ cities.

香港德邦物流有限公司 (Deppon Logistics HongKong) bridges the gap between international commerce and the world's largest manufacturing economy. Our Hong Kong platform provides importers, exporters, and e-commerce businesses with direct access to Deppon's unrivalled mainland network.

From Hong Kong's ports and airports through to any location in Guangdong, the Yangtze River Delta, or the furthest corners of China's interior, we provide end-to-end visibility, professional handling, and competitive transit times.
