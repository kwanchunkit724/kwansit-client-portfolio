About Summan

Built on Reliability,
Driven by Excellence

Summan Engineering Limited was founded in 2014 as a multi-disciplinary construction contractor headquartered in Jordan, Kowloon. Well-equipped to handle all kinds of small to large construction projects, the company has rapidly built a track record on some of Hong Kong's most iconic infrastructure and leisure projects.

Working alongside major clients including the Hong Kong International Airport Authority (HKIAA), CLP Power, and leading main contractors, Summan delivers quality across five core project categories: themed park works, marine works, temporary works, civil works and building works.

"Customer's satisfaction has always been our topmost priority — driven by expertise, commitment and due diligence in every project we undertake."

Multi-Disciplinary Capabilities

Summan Engineering delivers across six specialist construction disciplines, combining technical expertise with on-site experience across Hong Kong's most demanding projects.

🏗
Formwork & Concreting
High-quality formwork systems and concrete placement across commercial, institutional and infrastructure projects. Experienced in complex geometries, high-specification tolerances and large pours.
CIVIL · BUILDING
🔩
Metal Works & Scaffolding
Supply, erection and dismantling of metal scaffolding, working platforms and falsework for construction and maintenance. Experienced in complex scaffolding at height and difficult access locations.
TEMPORARY WORKS
🧱
Wet Trades
Full range of wet trade works including plastering, tiling, blockwork and associated finishes. Delivered across airports, cultural centres, residential and commercial properties to exacting standards.
BUILDING WORKS
