About Us

A Trusted Builder Since 2006

瑞群建築有限公司

瑞群建築有限公司於2006年9月12日由黃群先生成立。本公司高層管理成員曾承造過多棟大廈、別墅、廠房的新建、維修改善及加建工程。

In 2007, Managing Director Mr. Wong Kwan obtained the Buildings Department's accreditation as a Registered General Building Contractor (GBC 4/2007), cementing the company's technical expertise and professional standing. Following market demand, we later established a building maintenance division to participate actively in Hong Kong's building renovation sector.

Our quality management system has been validated through ISO9001:2008 certification — confirming our management approach and quality control systems meet the highest international standards. We remain committed to delivering excellent construction and building maintenance services with professionalism and integrity.

Our Services

Comprehensive Construction Services

全方位建築工程服務

From new construction to maintenance and professional planning, we provide end-to-end building services for residential, commercial, and institutional clients.

🏗️
New Works
新建工程
Full-scale construction of new buildings including residential blocks, villas, and industrial facilities. We manage all aspects from foundation to completion as the main contractor.
🔧
A&A Works
改建及加建工程
Alteration and Addition works for existing buildings. We handle structural modifications, extensions, and improvements while ensuring compliance with all relevant ordinances and regulations.
🏢
Maintenance Works
樓宇維修工程
Comprehensive building maintenance services for residential blocks and commercial properties. Active participant in Hong Kong Housing Society's "Operation Building Bright" programme.
🌍
Land Development
土地發展
We collaborate with developers on property acquisition, amalgamation, redevelopment, sales, building planning, and architectural design — introducing innovative and sustainable construction solutions.
📋
Pre-construction Planning
施工規劃
Our experienced building specialists provide project planning, construction method and sequencing, feasibility assessment, procurement and contract analysis, risk assessment, cost control, and safety/quality planning.
🎯
Construction Management
工程管理
With years of experience as a general contractor, we provide comprehensive project analysis and supervision — ensuring every stage proceeds safely and smoothly, in full compliance with the contract.
