About Us
Hong Kong's First Indoor Sports Complex

As the first international flagship of the JOYPOLIS SPORTS brand, JOYPOLIS SPORTS HONG KONG is located in the five-story Health and Wellness Centre at Kai Tak Sports Park, spanning nearly 30,000 square feet.

It offers dozens of exciting sports and entertainment facilities, bringing you an entirely new interactive sports experience. Whether you're an adult or a kid, visiting with family or friends, on a date, or simply passionate about health and fitness, you'll find joy and vitality here!

This all-weather indoor sports complex, equipped with cutting-edge technology and interactive facilities, guarantees a mix of fun and fitness for everyone — a place where you can work up a sweat, train your body, and enjoy endless fun.
