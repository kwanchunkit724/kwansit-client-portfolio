About Us

A Century of Building Excellence

Gammon Construction Limited is one of Hong Kong's most established and respected contractors, with roots stretching back to 1919. From the earliest days of Hong Kong's development to today's mega-infrastructure projects, Gammon has played a central role in shaping the city's built environment.

As a joint venture between Balfour Beatty plc — one of the world's leading infrastructure groups — and Jardine Matheson, one of Asia's most influential conglomerates, Gammon combines international expertise with deep local knowledge to deliver exceptional outcomes for clients across the public and private sectors.

Our integrated capabilities span building construction, civil and structural engineering, foundations and geotechnics, mechanical and electrical services, tunnelling, and virtual design and construction — making us a true one-stop solution for Hong Kong's most complex and demanding projects.
