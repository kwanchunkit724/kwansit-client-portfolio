Greenwood HK is a professional accounting and audit services firm based in Hong Kong, serving businesses across every stage of their growth — from startup incorporation to complex multi-entity audit engagements.

We combine deep technical expertise in Hong Kong accounting standards and tax regulations with a practical, client-focused approach. Our goal is simple: deliver accurate, timely, and valuable financial services that give you the confidence to make better business decisions.

Whether you need a statutory audit, monthly bookkeeping, or strategic tax advice, Greenwood HK has the expertise and commitment to deliver — with integrity at every step.
