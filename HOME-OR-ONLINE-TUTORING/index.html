Education Feature

Private Tutoring in Hong Kong

Private tutoring is a cornerstone of education in Hong Kong. We've spoken with leading tutoring centres to help you find the right support for your child — whether they need subject-specific help, learning differences support, or university application guidance.

Barty Education and Development (BartyED)
📍 Wan Chai, Hong Kong (est. 2016)
BartyED launched in 2016 and has grown from a small office to a large centre in Wan Chai. Their tutors — from Hong Kong, UK, Ireland, and international institutions including Oxford — support students from age 5 through university. They specialise in students with learning differences, mentoring, and subject-specific tutoring. Achievement: 9 of 11 Oxford/Cambridge applicants received offers in one sitting; 100% success rate placing students in UK boarding schools of their choice.
