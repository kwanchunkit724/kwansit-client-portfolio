Business Overview

Four Core Divisions

HKR International operates across four complementary business segments, creating a diversified portfolio that has delivered sustained value to shareholders and communities.

🏗️
Property Development
地產發展
HKR International has an outstanding track record in residential and commercial property development in Hong Kong and China, delivering prestigious projects that set benchmarks in design and quality.
  • Residential developments in prime locations
  • Commercial and mixed-use projects
  • Presence in Hong Kong and Mainland China
  • Landmark township development at Discovery Bay
🏨
Hospitality
酒店及旅遊
The Group's hospitality division encompasses hotel ownership and management across Hong Kong and Asia, offering guests exceptional service in distinctive, thoughtfully designed environments.
  • Panda Hotel — Tsuen Wan, Hong Kong
  • Hotel management expertise across Asia
  • Resort and leisure facilities
  • Discovery Bay Tourism Services
🏘️
Property Management
物業管理
Comprehensive property management services covering residential estates, commercial buildings, and clubhouse facilities, ensuring residents and tenants enjoy the highest standards of living and working environments.
  • Residential estate management
  • Commercial property management
  • Clubhouse and facilities management
  • Discovery Bay community management
🏥
Healthcare
醫療保健
HKR's healthcare division reflects the Group's commitment to improving quality of life, providing professional medical and health services to meet the growing demands of Hong Kong's ageing population.
  • Medical centre operations
  • Elderly care and rehabilitation services
  • Wellness and preventive healthcare
  • Healthcare facility management
Flagship Projects

Landmark Developments

HKR International's portfolio includes some of Hong Kong's most distinctive addresses, from self-contained townships to premier residential developments.

🏝️
Discovery Bay
愉景灣 · Lantau Island
Hong Kong's premier self-contained residential township on Lantau Island, home to over 20,000 residents. Discovery Bay offers a car-free, green living environment with extensive leisure, retail, and community facilities.
🐼
Panda Hotel
栢麗大道酒店 · Tsuen Wan
A well-established full-service hotel in the heart of Tsuen Wan, offering 1,026 guest rooms and suites alongside extensive conference, banquet, dining, and recreational facilities.
🌆
Luxury Residential
優質住宅發展 · Hong Kong Island
A series of prestigious residential developments across Hong Kong Island and the New Territories, delivering premium homes with thoughtful design, quality finishes, and prime locations.
Corporate History

Nearly Five Decades of Achievement

From its founding in 1977, HKR International has grown into one of Hong Kong's most respected and diversified property groups.

1977
Foundation
HKR International was established in Hong Kong by Dr. Cha Chi Ming, laying the foundation for a diversified property and hospitality group.
1982
Discovery Bay Development Begins
HKR commenced development of Discovery Bay on Lantau Island, a visionary residential township project that would transform the area into one of Hong Kong's most unique communities.
1988
Listed on Hong Kong Stock Exchange
HKR International achieved its listing on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (stock code: 0480), gaining access to public capital markets to fund continued growth.
1990s
Hospitality Expansion
Expanded the hospitality division with the development and operation of Panda Hotel in Tsuen Wan, complementing the Group's property development activities.
2000s
Mainland China Expansion
Extended property development activities into Mainland China, capitalising on the growing demand for quality residential and commercial properties.
Today
Continuing the Legacy
HKR International continues to develop premium properties, manage iconic assets, and deliver exceptional hospitality experiences across Hong Kong and Asia, building on nearly five decades of excellence.
