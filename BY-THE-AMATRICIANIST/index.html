"Italian cuisine is well-known everywhere for its use of fresh ingredients. It is a cuisine which is written in history and nobody can change it."
— Chef Paolo Monti, The Amatricianist

AMA Ristorante was created to deliver the experience of authentic Italian cuisine made from the best imported Italian ingredients in a comfortable, vibrant environment. Our Roman and Italian dishes celebrate seasonal produce — a philosophy Chef Paolo Monti has honed over 35 years in professional kitchens.

Our 25th floor venue offers plenty of natural light during the day and lively cityscape views at night, with a small outdoor area for alfresco moments. A large video wall, PA system and fully stocked bar complete the setting — ideal for both intimate dinners and memorable private events.

AMA Ristorante is the brainchild of Chef Paolo Monti — "The Amatricianist" — whose life's mission is to provide an authentically Roman Italian dining experience in Hong Kong.

Drawing on a lifetime career of cooking Italian food, Paolo grew up in a family where food was an essential part of daily living. His grandmother's side grew vegetables in the countryside and made cheeses and breads, while his grandfather's side cultivated grapes for the family's consumption.

Gaining all-around experience in the kitchen from a young age, Chef Paolo's menus focus on Italy's seasonal ingredients. He delivers his passion through creating light, delicious hand-made pastas and the finest fish and meat dishes — each prepared with the authenticity that only a Roman can bring.

"Authentic traditional Italian cuisine in Hong Kong, made with fresh ingredients and passionately prepared with a modern twist."
