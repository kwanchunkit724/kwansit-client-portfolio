Trusted Accounting & Tax Services

Yu & Co. CPA Limited provides a full range of professional accounting and corporate services, delivering practical and commercially-focused advice to help your business succeed.

📊
Audit & Assurance
Statutory audits and assurance engagements conducted by licensed CPAs, providing credible and independent verification of financial statements in compliance with Hong Kong auditing standards.
📝
Tax Compliance & Planning
Profits tax and salaries tax return preparation, tax planning advice and IRD enquiry handling, ensuring your business meets all tax obligations efficiently while minimising tax exposure.

Full bookkeeping and financial statement preparation services, keeping your accounts up to date and giving you the financial clarity needed to manage and grow your business.
