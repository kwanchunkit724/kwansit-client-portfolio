Our Story

Building Homes Around the World's Table

JIA Group is a Hong Kong-based food and beverage operator that has earned a reputation internationally as an award-winning trendsetter in the hospitality industry.

"As the world becomes more fractured, particularly in this digital age, we believe in the importance of spending more face to face quality time together with friends, family and people in general. Our vision is to build spaces in cities around the world that allow people to connect over a table."

With the opening of 208 Duecento Otto in 2010, JIA Group created a number of restaurants that led the dining trend in Hong Kong — one of the most competitive food and beverage markets in the world. Today, the group's portfolio includes iconic venues spanning Chinese, Thai, Italian, French, Spanish and contemporary cuisines.

JIA — the Chinese word for "home" — reflects the group's philosophy of creating spaces where guests feel a genuine sense of belonging, warmth, and connection. Every venue is crafted with style, substance and authenticity at its core.

Our vision is to build spaces in cities around the world that allow people to connect over a table and to add enjoyment into people's lives by allowing them to build a sense of home.
