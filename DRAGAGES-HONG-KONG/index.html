About Us

Delivering Excellence in Hong Kong

Dragages Hong Kong (香港寶嘉建築有限公司) is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Bouygues Construction, a global leader in building and civil works with operations across more than 60 countries. In Hong Kong, Dragages has established itself as a trusted partner for some of the city's most complex and prestigious construction projects.

With a presence spanning decades, we have contributed to landmark infrastructure including MTR rail lines, major airport works, cross-harbour tunnels, premium residential developments and government facilities. Our integrated capabilities, combined with the global knowledge base of the Bouygues Group, position us uniquely to tackle Hong Kong and Greater Bay Area's infrastructure challenges.

Also operating through 布依格土木工程有限公司 (Bouygues Civil Works Hong Kong Ltd), we deliver specialist civil engineering and tunnelling expertise across the region.
