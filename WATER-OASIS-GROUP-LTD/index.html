About Us

A Quarter-Century of Beauty Excellence

Founded in 1998 and listed on the Main Board of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong in 2002, Water Oasis Group has built a reputation as one of Hong Kong's most trusted and innovative names in the beauty industry.

The Group has expanded over the years into an esteemed provider of self-owned beauty services, along with a strong product portfolio of self-owned and licensed skincare brands. Serving clients across all major market segments, our Oasis brand family delivers a remarkably comprehensive all-round service offering unlike anything else in the market.

Already a leading player in Hong Kong, the Group is aiming to become a regional leader across Greater China.
