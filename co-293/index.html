About BFF

Focused on Customer Success

BFF International Freight Services Limited (先達國際貨運物流有限公司) is a Hong Kong-based international freight forwarder headquartered in San Po Kong, Kowloon. We specialize in providing comprehensive freight and logistics solutions for businesses of all sizes.

Our team of experienced freight agents offers tailored solutions covering air freight, sea freight, warehousing, trucking, and project cargo. We combine local expertise with a global network to ensure your cargo moves efficiently, safely, and on time.

We leverage our deep industry knowledge to navigate the complexities of international shipping, customs regulations, and supply chain management — keeping your cargo flowing and your business growing.

Our Core Value

"The core value of our team in Hong Kong is to focus on customer success and satisfaction. Our agents provide the most comprehensive freight solution for our customers, while our customer service team ensures a positive experience with BFF."
