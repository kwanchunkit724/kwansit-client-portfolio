About Osmaan Sharif

Your Guide on the Business Quest

Osmaan Sharif is a business performance coach who works with ambitious entrepreneurs to help them unlock their full potential and build businesses that create flow, freedom and fulfilment.

Through his unique Business Magic Formula™ and the powerful Wealth Dynamics profiling tool, Osmaan helps entrepreneurs identify their natural strengths, overcome limiting beliefs, and grow their businesses in a way that's authentic and energising.

The Framework

The Business Magic Formula™

Three interconnected elements that together create the conditions for business magic to happen.

M

Mindset

Build the beliefs, mental frameworks, and resilience that allow you to navigate challenges, reframe setbacks, and show up as your best self in business.

S

Strategy

Develop clear, focused strategies aligned with your Wealth Dynamics profile — doing more of what works for you and less of what drains your energy.

E

Energy

Protect and amplify your energy through smart working practices, delegation, and building a business that creates flow rather than friction.
