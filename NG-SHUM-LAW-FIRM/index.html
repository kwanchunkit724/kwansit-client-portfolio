About Ng & Shum

Premier Hong Kong Law Firm Since 1984

A full-service law firm with deep Hong Kong roots and an extensive global network, offering expert legal advice across dispute resolution, corporate law, and notarial services.

Ng & Shum was established in Hong Kong in 1984, celebrating its 40th anniversary in April 2024. Over four decades, the firm has built a reputation as a premier Hong Kong law firm that leverages its common law heritage with cross-border capabilities.

The firm combines deep local knowledge with an international perspective, advising clients on matters ranging from complex commercial disputes and arbitration to corporate transactions, China attestation, and international notarial services.

1984 Founded in Hong Kong by Steve Ng (Ng Siu Pang)
1992 Opened representative office in Guangzhou — among the first Hong Kong lawyers in mainland China
1994 Established the YangTze Law Network of affiliated overseas firms
2015 Founder established "YangTze Law," an England & Wales firm with Alternative Business Structure status
2017 Relocated mainland branch to Shenzhen to serve the Greater Bay Area

Our Founder

Multi-Jurisdictional Legal Leadership

Ng & Shum is led by its founder, a uniquely qualified practitioner holding concurrent legal qualifications across four jurisdictions.

⚖️

Steve Ng (Ng Siu Pang)

Founding Partner · Solicitor · Notary Public · China-Appointed Attesting Officer

Educated at the University of Liverpool, Steve Ng has built one of Hong Kong's most distinguished multi-jurisdictional legal careers. Admitted as a Hong Kong solicitor in 1981, he has since accumulated qualifications across four major common law and civil law jurisdictions, enabling Ng & Shum to advise clients with cross-border legal needs.

Beyond his legal practice, Steve Ng has made significant contributions to public life in Hong Kong, serving as a District Board member and participating in the Basic Law Consultative Committee and the Central Policy Unit — demonstrating the firm's deep commitment to the Hong Kong community.
