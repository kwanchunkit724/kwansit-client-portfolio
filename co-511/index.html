About Us

Hong Kong's Pallet & Logistics Equipment Specialist

"Supplying reliable, standards-compliant pallets and logistics equipment to keep Hong Kong's supply chains moving efficiently."
— Parlee Enterprises (HK) Co., Ltd.

Parlee Enterprises (HK) Co., Ltd. (栢利貿易香港有限公司) is a professional wholesale supplier of industrial pallets and material handling equipment in Hong Kong. We serve warehouses, manufacturers, freight forwarders, importers/exporters, and logistics operators who require reliable pallet supply.

Our product range includes American Standard pallets, Euro Standard pallets, plastic pallets, and custom-specification wooden pallets. With local Hong Kong stock, we offer fast delivery and competitive bulk pricing — ensuring your operations are never held up by pallet availability.
