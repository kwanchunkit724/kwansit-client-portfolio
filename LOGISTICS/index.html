About Us

Your Trusted Logistics Partner

Link Hong Logistics is a Hong Kong-based trucking and logistics company dedicated to delivering reliable, efficient freight solutions for businesses of all sizes. We specialize in local Hong Kong delivery, cross-border China-HK logistics, and cargo handling services.

With a customer-first approach and a commitment to on-time delivery, we serve clients across a wide range of industries. Whether you need regular scheduled runs or one-off cargo movement, our team is ready to provide a tailored solution for your needs.
