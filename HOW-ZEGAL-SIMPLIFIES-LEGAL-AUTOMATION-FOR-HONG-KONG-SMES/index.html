About Zegal

Where Law Meets Technology

Zegal transforms complex legal processes into automated, searchable, and manageable workflows — giving businesses the power of a legal team at a fraction of the cost.

Zegal is a legal technology platform that automates contract creation, management, and signing. With lawyer-reviewed templates and an AI-powered document builder, businesses can handle legal work in minutes — not months.

In Hong Kong's complex regulatory and commercial landscape, businesses face growing legal demands. Zegal democratises access to professional-grade legal tools, enabling SMEs, startups, and enterprises alike to operate with confidence and compliance.

Founded in Hong Kong and now operating across Asia and the UK, Zegal serves over 20,000 companies — from schools and law firms to financial institutions and technology companies.

How It Works

From Draft to Signed in Minutes

1
Choose a Template
