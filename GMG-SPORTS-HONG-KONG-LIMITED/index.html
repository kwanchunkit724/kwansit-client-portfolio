About Us

Inspiring Active
Lifestyles Globally

Mission: Dare to develop better products and experiences

GMG is a global retailer and distributor operating across MENA, Southeast Asia, and Hong Kong. Through its sports division — with approximately 450 stores worldwide — GMG inspires and empowers active lifestyles by distributing a diversified portfolio of leading athletic brands.

— GMG Group · GMG Sports Hong Kong Limited

GMG Sports Hong Kong Limited serves as the Group's Hong Kong entity, connecting world-class sports brands with consumers across Asia. With decades of retail expertise and a deep understanding of local and regional markets, GMG bridges global brands with active communities.
